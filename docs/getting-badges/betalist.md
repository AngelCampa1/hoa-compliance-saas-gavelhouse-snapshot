# BetaList Submission

Sources verified:

- https://betalist.com/criteria
- https://betalist.com/support

## Requirement Summary

- Product should be unreleased, private beta, or recently launched.
- Product should not already have significant press coverage.
- Startup must be a technology startup.
- Landing page must be distinct, decent-looking, and on its own domain.
- BetaList rejects free hosting subdomains and direct app-store/social links.
- Visitors must be able to sign up, download, log in, or get access.
- Editorial review can take weeks.
- If selected, acceptance email usually arrives within about a week, and
  regular accepted submissions may take about two months to be featured.
- Priority listing is a paid faster-review option that can move a submission
  toward the front of the review queue, but it does not guarantee acceptance.

## Eligibility Decision

Use the `recently launched` angle only after confirming it is still accurate at
submission time. Do not call Gavelhouse mature. Do not call it private beta
because SaaSHub and G2 require a released or available product.

## Acceptance Risk Notes

- Strong fit if still recently launched at submission time: technology startup,
  custom domain, clear access path, real product page, and clear early-adopter
  feedback story.
- Risk: BetaList prefers very new or pre-launch products. Submit promptly while
  the recently launched framing is still true.
- Risk: B2B vertical SaaS for HOA boards is less broad than a typical consumer
  early-adopter tool. Keep the copy focused on the unusual workflow and direct
  founder feedback.
