screenshots, features, likes, comments, and user contributions. The exact
submission form may require an account, so use this as the prepared field pack
and verify any account-only fields during the contribution flow.

## Submission Positioning

Use Gavelhouse as an alternative to HOA/community management tools where the
buyer is a self-managed board, not a professional property manager.

Description:

```text
Gavelhouse is an alternative to general HOA management tools when the buyer is a self-managed volunteer board. Instead of starting with rental workflows or management-company operations, Gavelhouse starts with the board record: reserve and operating fund separation, state requirement tracking, dues records, meeting and governance workflows, owner visibility, and audit packet exports. It is best for self-managed HOA and condo communities up to 500 units that need predictable flat pricing and clearer records without per-unit fees.
```
