## Long Descriptions

### SaaS Directory

Self-managed HOA boards often outgrow spreadsheets before they are ready to
hire a management company. Gavelhouse gives those boards one place for reserve
fund separation, state-specific requirement tracking, dues records, governance
workflows, owner visibility, and board-ready reports. It is built for volunteer
HOA and condo boards that want an operating record for the association, not a
rental-property system with per-door pricing. Plans are flat by community size,
billed annually with Y80OFF, and start at $10/mo with Y80OFF. Gavelhouse is an operating tool, not
legal counsel.

### Alternative / Review Site

Gavelhouse is a compliance-first alternative to general HOA management tools
and property-management platforms when the buyer is a self-managed volunteer
board. Instead of starting with rental workflows or management-company
operations, Gavelhouse starts with the board record: reserve and operating fund
separation, state requirement tracking, dues records, meeting and governance
workflows, owner visibility, and audit packet exports. It is best for
self-managed HOA and condo communities up to 500 units that need predictable
flat pricing and clearer records without per-unit fees.

### Product Hunt

Most self-managed HOA boards run on a mix of spreadsheets, email threads,
bank statements, and institutional memory. That works until a treasurer changes,
an owner asks for records, a reserve study deadline comes due, or the board
needs to prove what happened. Gavelhouse turns that loose record into one
board-ready operating system: reserve and operating fund separation, state rule
tracking, dues records, governance workflows, owner visibility, and reports.
It is built for volunteer boards, not property managers, with flat pricing and
direct founder support while the product is still young.

### BetaList

Gavelhouse is a recently launched product for self-managed HOA and condo boards
that need a clearer way to run the association record. Confirm that launch
framing is still accurate before submitting to BetaList. It combines finance,
reserve compliance, governance, owner operations, and reports in one place so
volunteer boards can stop handing the next treasurer a scattered pile of
spreadsheets and inbox history. Early users can start a trial, try the board
workflow, and send feedback directly to Angel Campa, the builder.
