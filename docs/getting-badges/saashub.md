# SaaSHub Submission

Source verified: https://www.saashub.com/services/submit

## Requirement Summary

- Product must be released. SaaSHub rejects products that are not released yet.
- Product cannot be a waitlist-only landing page.
- Product must use a real domain, not a free subdomain.
- Product must be in English.
- Submit a product website or product page URL.
- Add categories.
- List competitors. Missing competitors slows review and moves the submission
  down the queue.
- Verify the product with an email address on the product domain for higher
  priority.
