## Requirement Summary

- G2 categorizes B2B software and services.
- G2 categorization is based on functionality and market fit.
- G2's current public product-information docs require product and vendor
  names, product URL, product category, and profile assets.
- Software companies should have one listing per product.
- Product and vendor names should match the website and should not include
  marketing terms, keywords, or symbols.
- Products are categorized by functionality, not just audience.
- Category requests should include evidence such as demos, blog content,
  presentations, or product pages.
- Profile logo must be at least 400px and JPG, PNG, or GIF.
- Grid logo must be SVG and under 5MB.
- Profile banner image should be 1260x240. G2 recommends 2500x476 if the
  banner renders poorly.

## Eligibility Decision

Submit Gavelhouse as generally available B2B software, not as private beta.
If the form asks for stage, avoid `alpha`, `beta`, or waitlist language because
G2 is a review marketplace for available products. Use `recently launched` or
`generally available`.

## Review Collection Plan

G2 is not useful until real users review the product. Use this only after the
listing exists and Gavelhouse has real evaluators or users who have completed a
meaningful action.

1. Build a list of 20 real users or board evaluators.
2. Ask only people who have actually used Gavelhouse.
3. Send a personal note with the direct G2 review URL.
4. Offer a small thank-you only if it complies with current G2 policy and is
   disclosed where required.
5. Follow up once after 5 days.
6. Target 10 quality reviews first, then 20 reviews for stronger badge
   eligibility.

## Caveats

- Do not submit this as a beta or waitlist product. Position it as recently
  launched and available to use.
- Request `Community Association Management Software` as the primary category.
  Use accounting, financial reporting, document management, and community
  management only as supporting evidence if G2 asks for alternatives.
- Do not claim a maintenance-request workflow unless current product evidence
  is added before submission.
- Do not create multiple profiles for pricing tiers.
- Do not claim G2 badges before they are earned.
- Do not ask for fake reviews or reviews from people who have not used the
  product.
