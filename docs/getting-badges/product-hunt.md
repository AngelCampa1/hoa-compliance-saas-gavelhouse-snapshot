## Requirement Summary

  accepted.

- Description max: 500 characters.
- Choose up to 3 launch tags.
- Thumbnail is required, square dimensions recommended at 240x240, under 3MB.
- Gallery needs at least 2 images before it is viewable. Recommended size:
  1270x760.
- Video is optional; only YouTube links are supported, and the video must not
  be private.
- First comment is strongly recommended and should ask for feedback, not
  upvotes.
- Product Hunt lets makers schedule up to 1 month ahead.

## First Comment

```text
Hey Product Hunt,

I built Gavelhouse for self-managed HOA and condo boards that are trying to run real association operations without turning the whole thing into a pile of spreadsheets and inbox history.

The first problem I wanted to make hard to ignore was the reserve record. A volunteer board needs to know which dollars are operating funds, which dollars are reserve funds, what state requirements apply, and what report they would hand to the next treasurer, an owner, a lender, or an auditor.

Gavelhouse now brings that into one workflow:

- Reserve and operating fund separation
- State-specific compliance tracking
- Dues records and owner visibility
- Meeting and governance records
- Board-ready reports and audit packet exports
- Flat pricing without per-unit fees

It is not legal advice. It is the operating record a board can actually keep up to date.

I would love feedback on two things: whether the reserve-compliance positioning is clear from the first screen, and what a volunteer board would need to see before trusting a new product with its records.
```
