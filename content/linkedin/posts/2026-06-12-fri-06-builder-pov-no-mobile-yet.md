Gavelhouse tracks "no native mobile app yet" as an honest con. The web interface works on mobile browsers.

We are comfortable saying that plainly.

For self-managed boards, the highest-risk jobs are reserve separation, compliance deadlines, governance records, owner requests, reporting, and month-end close. Those workflows need accuracy and auditability before they need another icon on a phone.

A mobile app may make sense later.

Right now, we would rather make the core board record more dependable.
