Gavelhouse tracks New Jersey's separate account requirement and baseline funding rule that the reserve balance can never drop below zero.

Those two ideas work together.

Separate accounts help protect reserve money from ordinary operating use. Baseline funding discipline helps keep the balance from being exhausted by projects, timing mistakes, or underfunded budgets.

Boards should review both before approving capital work.

Where is the money held? What happens to the reserve balance after the project? What does the record show?
