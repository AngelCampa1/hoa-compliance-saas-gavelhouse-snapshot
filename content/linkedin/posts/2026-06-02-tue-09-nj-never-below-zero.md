New Jersey is listed in Gavelhouse's compliance research as a disclosure state, but the reserve rule is not soft in practice.

The requirement says N.J.S.A. 46:8B-19 requires separate accounts. It also says the reserve balance can never drop below zero, with S2760 (2024) and S3992 (2025) establishing baseline funding rules.

That creates a simple board question:

Can the approved budget, planned projects, and reserve balance all coexist without pushing the fund negative?

If the answer is unclear, the board has an operations problem before it has a communications problem. Homeowners will eventually see the impact through dues, assessments, or delayed work.

The finance workflow should catch that before the meeting packet goes out.
