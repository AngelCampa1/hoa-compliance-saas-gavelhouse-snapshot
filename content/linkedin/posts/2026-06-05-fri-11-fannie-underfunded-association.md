An individual owner can do everything right.

Pay dues on time. Follow the rules. Keep the unit or home in good shape.

Then a buyer's loan hits association review, and the reserve question becomes part of the transaction.

The requirement says underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals once Fannie Mae's 15% reserve allocation rule takes effect for Full Review applications on January 4, 2027.

That is one reason reserve funding is more than a board finance issue.

It affects owners who may have no control over the board's prior budget decisions.

Boards should treat that as part of fiduciary duty. The reserve plan goes beyond future repairs. It is also part of keeping homes inside the association financeable.
