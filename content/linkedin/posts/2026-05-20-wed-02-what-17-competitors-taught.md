I looked at 17 HOA software products before deciding to build Gavelhouse. Here's the gap I kept seeing.

Tools built for professional management companies: AppFolio, Buildium, CINC Systems, Vantaca. Powerful. Expensive. Built around the assumption of staff accountants and management fees.

Per-unit tools with basic features: EasyHOA, Effortless HOA, Vinteum. Inexpensive. No reserve compliance. Solve the dues collection problem, not the compliance problem.

Website builders and portals: HOA Express. Not accounting software.

Payment processors: ClickPay. Not management software.

Tools with QuickBooks dependencies: HOALife, Vinteum. Outsource the accounting problem to a tool that doesn't solve it.

And then a middle tier of tools that handle communications and documents reasonably well but treat reserve compliance as a secondary feature, if they include it at all.

The gap was specific: no platform was built from the ground up for self-managed volunteer boards with compliance as the primary design constraint, at a price a volunteer board could approve without a lengthy discussion.

That gap is what Gavelhouse is for. Whether we fill it well is something the market will determine. But the gap is real, and I kept seeing it in every tool I evaluated.
