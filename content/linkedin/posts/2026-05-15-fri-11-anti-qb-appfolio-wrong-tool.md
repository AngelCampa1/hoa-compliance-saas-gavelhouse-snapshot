AppFolio starts at $280/month plus $0.80-$5 per unit plus a $400+ setup fee. It is built for professional management companies, not volunteer boards.

A 100-unit community on AppFolio's minimum pricing would pay around $280 base plus $80-$500 in per-unit fees plus the setup fee. That is $360-$780 per month for a tool built for a professional management company running a portfolio of communities.

The self-managed board does not get volume pricing. It gets enterprise pricing designed for a different use case.

AppFolio is a capable platform for professional management companies. It has features those companies need: multi-community portfolio management, professional accounting, owner portals for rental properties. A self-managed volunteer board does not use most of those features.

More important for compliance: AppFolio's setup and workflow assumes a professional management company is configuring and operating it. The self-managed treasurer trying to set up AppFolio for a 75-unit HOA is not the intended user, and the platform's complexity reflects that.

Gavelhouse's Growth plan covers 51-200 homes at $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF. No per-unit fees. No setup fee. Built for the volunteer board treasurer, not for a professional management company.
