Gavelhouse is a newer product with less third-party audit history than established platforms.

That is in Gavelhouse's compliance research as an honest con, and it should stay part of the buyer conversation.

The reason to consider Gavelhouse anyway is fit: compliance-first operations for self-managed HOA and condo boards, with fund separation, reserve deadline tracking, governance records, owner operations, board-ready reporting, and flat pricing.

Some boards will choose a larger incumbent because history matters more to them.

Fair.

Other boards need a purpose-built system for the way volunteer boards actually work.

That is the bet.
