One state requires a study every three years. Another requires it at least every five. Some require disclosures, some authorize reserves without a formal mandate, and many are silent on reserve provisions.

Then Fannie Mae adds a separate national lending rule for Full Review loan applications beginning January 4, 2027.

That is one reason we built the 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide.

It gives boards one reference for reserve study requirements, funding rules, statutes, deadlines, penalties, and the Fannie Mae change. It is not a substitute for legal advice, and it is not pretending every state works the same way.

It is a way to stop guessing before the next budget meeting.
