Some states authorize or encourage reserves without mandating a formal study. Others are silent.

That does not remove the Fannie Mae deadline.

The requirement is explicit: LL-2026-03 applies to all associations regardless of state law, with a 15% reserve allocation requirement for Full Review loan applications starting January 4, 2027.

For boards in states without a detailed reserve statute, this can feel like a surprise from outside the HOA code.

It is still a board operations issue. Homeowner financing can depend on it.
