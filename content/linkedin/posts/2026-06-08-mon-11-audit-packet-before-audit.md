The worst time to assemble an audit packet is after a question lands.

At that point, the board is searching for bank records, reserve balances, approved budgets, owner request history, meeting decisions, and explanations for transfers.

Gavelhouse's product source names audit-packet exports and month-end close with attested balances in the Scale plan.

The point is not to create a fancy file.

The point is to make the board's financial position explainable before there is pressure. A clean record protects the board's time and gives the next volunteer a better starting point.
