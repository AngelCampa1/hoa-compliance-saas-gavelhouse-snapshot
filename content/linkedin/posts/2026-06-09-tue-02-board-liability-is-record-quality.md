Volunteer boards make imperfect decisions. That is normal.

The bigger problem is making decisions without a record the board can explain later.

Why was reserve funding set at that level? Which study did the board rely on? Were operating and reserve funds separated? What did owners request, and how did the board respond?

Those questions need answers outside someone's memory.

The compliance research's trust signals include true fund accounting, board-ready reporting, audit packs, month-end close, governance workflows, and owner visibility.

That is the shape of a defensible operating record.
