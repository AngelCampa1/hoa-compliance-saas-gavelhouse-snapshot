January 4, 2027 is the date boards should have circled.

That is when Fannie Mae's 15% reserve allocation requirement for Full Review loan applications takes effect under LL-2026-03.

The board-level question is not abstract.

Can we show the allocation? Can we explain where the reserve money sits? Can we prove it is separate from operating cash? Can we pull the record without asking one volunteer to search their files?

If the answer is "probably," that is not a system.

The reserve posture needs to be visible before an owner's loan file depends on it.
