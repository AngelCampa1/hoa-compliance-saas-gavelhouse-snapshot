Most boards do not know their reserve allocation percentage. That becomes a problem January 4, 2027.

Fannie Mae's Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% floor. Reserve contributions must equal at least 15% of total assessment income for the community to pass a Full Review lender check.

The number itself is not complicated. It is one line divided by another line on a budget document that already exists.

The problem is that most self-managed boards have never framed their budget in those terms. They know what dues are. They know what the reserve contribution is in dollars. They have not thought about it as a percentage of total income.

After January 4, 2027, lenders will think about it exactly that way.

If you are on a volunteer board and no one has flagged this yet, here is the shortest version of what to do:

Take your current annual reserve contribution. Divide it by your total annual assessment revenue. Multiply by 100. That is your percentage.

If it is below 15%, you have a decision to make before your next budget cycle.
