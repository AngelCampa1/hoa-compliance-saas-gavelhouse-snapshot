When owners cannot see where a request stands, they ask again.

Then another board member answers. Then someone forwards a thread. Then the secretary has to reconstruct the record later.

That is avoidable.

Gavelhouse tracks owner portal request visibility and audit trail as part of Gavelhouse's Growth plan. The operational reason is simple: a request should have a status, a history, and a record the board can rely on.

Self-managed boards do not have spare admin capacity. Re-answering the same question from scattered threads is a bad use of volunteer time.
