Maryland is one of the states where reserve separation cannot be treated as a board preference.

Gavelhouse tracks Maryland's reserve account separation requirement and a $10,000 fine as the highest explicit monetary penalty in the state data.

For a volunteer board, that should change the tone of the conversation.

This is not about having a cleaner chart of accounts. It is about whether reserve dollars can be identified, protected, and reviewed apart from operating cash.

The accounting setup has to match the legal exposure.
