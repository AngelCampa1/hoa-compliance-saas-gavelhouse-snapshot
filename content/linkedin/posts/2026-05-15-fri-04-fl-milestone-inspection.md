Florida condo buildings 30 years old or older require a milestone structural inspection. Near the coast, the trigger is 25 years.

The milestone inspection requirement came from Florida's Surfside reforms, initially in SB 4-D (2022) and extended by HB 913 (2025). It applies to residential condo buildings that meet the age threshold.

Phase 1 of the milestone inspection is a visual inspection by a licensed engineer or architect. If Phase 1 identifies concerns, Phase 2 requires a more detailed investigation, which may involve physical testing of structural elements.

The milestone inspection is separate from and in addition to the Structural Integrity Reserve Study requirement. A condo board with a building that meets the age threshold needs both: the inspection at the applicable age milestone, and the SIRS every 10 years for the ongoing reserve planning.

Florida Chapter 718 (condos) and the associated administrative rules govern the inspection timeline and reporting requirements. The reports must be maintained and, for the SIRS, the funding plan based on the study must be adopted by the board.

Florida's post-Surfside framework is the most comprehensive structural safety and reserve compliance regime in the country. If your Florida condo board is still mapping out what applies to your building, the milestone inspection timeline is the first place to check.
