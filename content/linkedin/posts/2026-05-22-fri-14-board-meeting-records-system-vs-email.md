Board meeting decisions that live only in someone's email thread are not really on the record.

This is one of the most common governance gaps in self-managed HOAs. The board makes a decision. Someone sends a summary email. Three board members reply. The decision is documented, sort of, in an email thread that nobody can find a year later when a new board member is asking why a vendor contract was approved.

What belongs in the system, not in email:

- Approved meeting minutes (draft and final)
- Board resolutions and votes
- Vendor contract approvals and the authorization dates
- Reserve study adoption and any waivers
- Special assessment authorizations
- Architectural request decisions

What email is fine for:

- Coordinating logistics for the next meeting
- Sharing a draft for comment before the vote
- Follow-up conversations after a decision is already documented

The test is simple: if someone joined the board today and needed to understand a decision the board made 18 months ago, could they find it in three minutes? If the answer is no, the records belong in a better place.
