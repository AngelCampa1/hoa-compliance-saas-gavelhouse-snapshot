Gavelhouse has honest cons in Gavelhouse's compliance research.

Newer product with less third-party audit history than established platforms. No native mobile app yet, though the web interface works on mobile browsers. Purpose-built for self-managed boards, not professional management companies managing many communities.

I like having those written down.

It keeps the product honest and makes the positioning sharper.

If a management company needs a mature enterprise platform with years of third-party audit history, Gavelhouse is not the obvious answer. If a volunteer board needs fund separation, reserve deadline tracking, owner operations, governance records, payments, and cleaner handoff, that is the problem we are choosing.

Specific tradeoffs are better than vague claims.

Boards deserve to know the fit before they spend time evaluating software.
