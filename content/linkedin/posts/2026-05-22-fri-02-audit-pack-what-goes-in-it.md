What exactly goes in an HOA audit pack, and when should you download one?

An audit pack is the set of financial records your board needs when an accountant, auditor, or state reviewer asks to see the books. For most self-managed boards, this means:

- General ledger for the period
- Bank statements with reconciliation
- Dues register showing collections and delinquencies
- Reserve fund balance and year-to-date activity
- Meeting minutes authorizing major expenditures

The "when" question is just as important as the "what." You need one at fiscal year-end. You need one when the board changes hands. You need one any time an owner or their attorney requests a financial review.

Waiting until someone asks is the wrong approach. If the records aren't clean and current, assembling an audit pack under pressure is painful.
