Four things every HOA treasurer should confirm before closing the books each month.

1. Bank statement matches the ledger. Pull the statement, reconcile line by line, flag anything that doesn't clear.

2. All dues payments are posted. Partial payments and returned checks have a way of sitting in limbo. Check the dues register before you sign off.

3. Reserve and operating funds are still separate. Commingling is how boards end up in legal trouble. Confirm the balances are in the right buckets.

4. Reports are attached and attested. Month-end is not done until someone with authority has reviewed the numbers and put their name on it.

If any of these four steps is skipped, the next treasurer inherits a mess. If all four are skipped for a year, the board has an audit exposure problem.
