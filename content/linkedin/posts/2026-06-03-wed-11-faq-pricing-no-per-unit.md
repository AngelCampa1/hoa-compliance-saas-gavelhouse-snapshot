Gavelhouse describes Gavelhouse pricing as flat plan tiers with no per-unit fees.

That is intentional.

Self-managed boards do not usually have procurement teams. They have volunteers trying to get a budget approved in a meeting where everyone already has opinions about dues.

Per-unit pricing can turn a software discussion into a unit-count negotiation. It also makes the cost feel like it grows just because the community is doing its normal job.

Gavelhouse's self-serve plans are based on community size bands, with annual pricing shown from Starter through Scale. Portfolio is custom for larger or multi-community operators. The operating idea is simple: give boards a cost they can understand before the meeting.

The product still has to earn the budget line.

But the board should not need a calculator to find the price.
