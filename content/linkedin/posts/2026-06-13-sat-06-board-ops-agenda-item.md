Boards do not need to debate reserves at every meeting. They do need a regular place to review them.

A standing agenda item can be simple:

Current reserve balance. Current operating balance. Any transfers since last meeting. Reserve study status. Upcoming capital work. State deadline changes. Fannie Mae 2027 readiness.

That list takes minutes when records are clean.

It takes an hour when the board has to reconstruct the facts from bank statements, old emails, and a spreadsheet no one wants to touch.
