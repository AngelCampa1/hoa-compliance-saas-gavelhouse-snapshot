The cleanest reserve policy in the world still depends on a human being handing over the work.

When a volunteer treasurer leaves, the next person needs more than a spreadsheet. They need bank access, authorized signer records, reserve account details, the current budget, the latest reserve study, open invoices, delinquency notes, and the next compliance dates.

Most of that is boring.

That is exactly why it gets missed.

The risk is not always fraud or neglect. Sometimes it is a board that cannot prove what was approved, what was paid, what belongs to operating, and what belongs to reserves.

Board operations should make the handoff survivable. If the system only works while one person remembers the setup, it is not really a system.
