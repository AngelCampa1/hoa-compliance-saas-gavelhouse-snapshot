Some states in Gavelhouse's compliance research have no specific reserve provisions. Boards in those states may be used to handling reserves as a local governance choice.

Fannie Mae changes the conversation.

LL-2026-03 creates a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications starting January 4, 2027. It applies regardless of state law.

That means a board can be legally quiet under its state statute and still create a financing problem for homeowners.

Reserve planning belongs on the agenda before that happens.
