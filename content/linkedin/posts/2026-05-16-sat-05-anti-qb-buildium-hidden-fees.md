Buildium advertises $62-$400/month. The actual cost after add-ons is 30-50% higher. Self-managed boards are not the target customer.

Buildium is built for professional property management companies. The platform has the features those companies need: tenant management, maintenance coordination, multi-property portfolios, owner portals. A self-managed HOA board uses a fraction of those features.

The 30-50% hidden fees on top of base price come from add-ons that professional management companies need and purchase as standard. For a self-managed board, those fees are for features you are unlikely to use, but the subscription structure makes them difficult to avoid.

Like AppFolio, Buildium's pricing and architecture assumes a professional operator. The self-managed volunteer board trying to configure Buildium for a 60-unit HOA is going against the grain of what the platform was designed for.

The reserve compliance problem for self-managed boards is structural. Buildium is designed for professional operators who already have accounting staff to manage fund separation manually. That's a different workflow from a volunteer treasurer handling their first close.

Gavelhouse's Growth plan is $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF for 51-200 homes. No hidden fees, no per-unit charges, no add-ons required for basic reserve compliance. Built for the self-managed board, not for a management company portfolio.
