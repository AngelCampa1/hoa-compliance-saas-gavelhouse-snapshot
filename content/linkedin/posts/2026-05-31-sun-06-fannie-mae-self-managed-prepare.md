What should a self-managed board do today to prepare for the Fannie Mae 2027 rule?

Fannie Mae's Lender Letter LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027. Full Review loan applications will require 15% reserve allocation. Seven months remain.

Here is a practical checklist for self-managed volunteer boards.

First: calculate your current reserve allocation percentage. Pull last year's approved budget. Divide reserve contributions by total assessment income. If the result is below 15%, you have a gap to close.

Second: determine your next budget cycle timeline. Most communities set budgets in Q4. If you need a dues increase to hit 15%, the board resolution needs to happen before the budget is finalized and homeowners need 30 to 60 days notice of a dues change in most states.

Third: document your reserve fund balance and contribution history. When a lender sends an HOA questionnaire, the certification needs to reflect accurate, current figures. If that information lives in a spreadsheet someone built three years ago, you have a documentation problem.

Fourth: tell your homeowners. The Fannie Mae rule makes reserve allocation a property value and mortgage eligibility question. Homeowners have a direct stake in whether the board closes the gap.
