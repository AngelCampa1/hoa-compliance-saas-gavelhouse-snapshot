A new board member can join in good faith and still inherit a messy reserve problem.

Maybe the last study is old. Maybe operating and reserve cash were tracked in the same tool. Maybe the board cannot find the prior disclosure. Maybe a project was approved without checking the funding plan.

That is one reason onboarding should include the reserve file, alongside meeting norms.

New directors need the current reserve study, the next required update date, recent balances, the approved allocation, and any state-specific requirement the board is tracking.

Good governance starts with knowing what you inherited.
