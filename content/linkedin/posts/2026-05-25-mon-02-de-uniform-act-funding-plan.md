Delaware Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act §81-318 requires associations to conduct a reserve study at least every five years.

The requirement does not stop at the study itself. The study must include a funding plan.

A funding plan is not a vague intention to collect dues. It specifies how the association will accumulate enough money to cover each reserve component before it reaches the end of its useful life.

If your Delaware HOA has a reserve study that is more than five years old, or has a study with no funding plan attached, the board is not in compliance with §81-318.

Volunteer boards often complete a study once and consider the obligation fulfilled. The five-year cycle means the study is a recurring responsibility, not a one-time box to check.
