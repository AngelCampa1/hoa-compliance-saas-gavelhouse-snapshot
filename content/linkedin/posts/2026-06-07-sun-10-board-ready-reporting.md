A report is not board-ready just because it exports to PDF.

Board-ready means the treasurer can explain what the numbers mean, where reserve money sits, what is owed, what changed since the last close, and what the board needs to decide.

Gavelhouse names "Board-ready reporting, audit packs, and month-end close" as a trust signal for Gavelhouse.

That wording matters. The output has to fit the meeting, the audit file, and the next volunteer who inherits the role.

Good reporting reduces confusion. It does not add another artifact to decode.
