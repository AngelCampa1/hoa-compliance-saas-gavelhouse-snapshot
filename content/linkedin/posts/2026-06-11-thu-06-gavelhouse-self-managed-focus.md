That product choice matters.

Professional management companies usually need portfolio workflows, staff assignment, vendor operations, and reporting across many associations. Self-managed boards have a different problem: volunteers need financial control, governance records, owner operations, and compliance deadlines in one place.

Gavelhouse defines Gavelhouse's target audience as self-managed volunteer HOA and condo boards in communities up to 500 units.

That focus keeps the product honest. A volunteer treasurer should not need enterprise software just to separate operating and reserve funds correctly.
