The target audience in Gavelhouse's compliance research is specific: self-managed volunteer HOA and condo boards in communities up to 500 units.

That does not mean every community under 500 units has the same needs.

A 35-home HOA may care most about dues, reserve separation, records, and treasurer handoff. A 300-unit condo may need stricter audit packets, owner request visibility, month-end close, and reserve study deadline tracking.

The common thread is not size alone.

It is the self-managed board trying to run financial control, governance, and owner operations without turning into a management company.

Gavelhouse is built around that operating reality.

If your board has professional staff and a large portfolio operation, it is probably the wrong fit.
