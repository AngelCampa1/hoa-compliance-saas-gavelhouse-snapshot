Dues are more than monthly cash coming in.

For a self-managed board, dues fund daily operations and long-term reserve obligations. If the board cannot see payment status clearly, it is harder to know whether the budget is actually supporting the reserve plan.

Gavelhouse tracks dues tracking, online payments with receipts, automated reminders, delinquency tracking, and reserve fund separation across Gavelhouse plans.

Those belong together.

A board should not have one system for owner payments and a separate side calculation for whether reserves are being funded.

The financial picture needs to match the board's obligations.
