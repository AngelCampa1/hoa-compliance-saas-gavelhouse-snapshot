What does "compliance-first" actually mean? Here is the plain-language answer.

Most HOA software is built around the workflows that management companies care about: owner portals, maintenance tickets, mass communications, and billing automation. Compliance is handled downstream, usually by whoever files the state disclosures.

Compliance-first means we built the architecture around the compliance obligation first, and put the workflow on top of that.

For Gavelhouse, that means three things specifically.

Reserve and operating funds are structurally separate. There is no way to mix them accidentally. The separation is enforced at the data layer, not by convention or by hoping the treasurer stays disciplined.

Reserve contribution history is accurate and reportable. You can see what was contributed to reserves in any period, generate a reserve allocation percentage, and produce documentation that survives a lender questionnaire under Fannie Mae's LL-2026-03 rules effective January 4, 2027.

Financial workflows produce board-ready outputs. Minutes, disclosures, and reports are formatted for the people who need them: board members, homeowners, and lenders. Not for accountants.

That is compliance-first. The statutory requirements are the architecture, not an afterthought.
