Some states authorize or encourage reserves without a formal mandate.

That does not make reserve planning optional as a board responsibility.

Major components still age. Owners still need a funding plan they can understand. Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve allocation requirement still applies to all associations regardless of state law, under Gavelhouse's compliance research.

If your board is in a permissive state, use the 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide as a starting point.

Know the state baseline. Then look at what your buildings, budget, and lender environment require.

Find it at gavelhouse.app/free/
