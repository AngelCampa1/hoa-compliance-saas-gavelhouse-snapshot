January 4, 2027 sounds like a lending deadline. For boards, it is really a budgeting deadline.

Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 raises the reserve allocation floor to 15% for Full Review loan applications. That applies to associations in every state, even where state law does not require a formal reserve study.

The practical question is simple: can the board point to the reserve allocation in the budget and explain it?

If the answer is "we will figure it out when a lender asks," the board is already late.
