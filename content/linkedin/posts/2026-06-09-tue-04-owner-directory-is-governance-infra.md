An owner directory sounds basic until it is wrong.

Then notices miss people. Dues questions go to stale contacts. Record requests take longer. Board communication gets patched together from old lists.

The compliance research includes core homeowner directory and governance records in Gavelhouse's Starter plan.

That pairing makes sense. Owner identity and board records belong together because board work depends on knowing who is connected to each property and what communication or request history exists.

Small data hygiene problems become governance problems when the board needs a reliable record.
