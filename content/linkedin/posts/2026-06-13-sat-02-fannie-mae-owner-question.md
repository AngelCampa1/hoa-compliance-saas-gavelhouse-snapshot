Picture the owner question in late 2026:

"Will our association meet Fannie Mae's new reserve requirement?"

The board should be able to answer without improvising. LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications starting January 4, 2027.

The answer should point to the approved budget, reserve allocation, current reserve record, and any reserve study or funding plan the board uses.

If the board cannot answer cleanly, the work is not merely financial. It is communication.
