Condo Control is around $49/month plus per-unit module fees. Partial reserve tracking. No dedicated reserve study module.

The per-unit module structure means the actual cost depends on what modules your community needs. For communities that need comprehensive reserve compliance, that means adding modules on top of the base price.

Partial reserve tracking through accounting is not the same as a dedicated reserve study module. Reserve tracking tells you what's in the account. A reserve study module determines what should be in the account based on component lifespans, replacement costs, and state-specific funding requirements.

A community that tracks their reserve account balance without a reserve study is measuring the wrong thing. The balance may look fine while the funding level relative to projected needs is critically low.

The percent-funded calculation requires the reserve study data: what components need replacement, when, and at what cost. That data produces the funding target. The account balance divided by the funding target is percent funded.

Without a dedicated reserve study module, communities are tracking money in and money out without the benchmark to know whether they're on track.

For communities in states with mandatory reserve study requirements, partial tracking leaves them short of compliance.

Know what your software actually produces before assuming your reserves are covered.
