Every state has different reserve fund requirements. Most boards only know their state's rules after they've already violated them.

California requires a 30-year reserve plan, annual visual inspections, and a full study every three years. Florida mandates structural integrity reserve studies for condos three stories and above, every 10 years. Maryland has a $10,000 commingling penalty. New Jersey's reserve balance can never drop below zero. Washington requires separate accounts by statute.

And starting January 4, 2027, Fannie Mae requires 15% reserve allocation for Full Review loans, regardless of state law.
