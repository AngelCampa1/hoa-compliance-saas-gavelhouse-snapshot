The PDF is the starting point.

The process is everything the board does after it: budget review, contribution decisions, account separation, owner disclosures, deadline tracking, project approvals, and handoff to the next treasurer.

That is where self-managed boards often lose the plot.

They paid for the study. They can find the file. But the annual budget no longer matches the funding plan, or nobody knows when the next update is due.

Reserve work has to become operations.
