California Civil Code 5510 explicitly prohibits commingling reserve and operating funds. The compliance research also lists penalties of $100 to $500 per day.

That should make boards careful about how they describe their accounting setup.

"We track reserves in a spreadsheet" is not the same as fund separation. "The treasurer knows which money is which" is not a control. "We only move money when needed" is not a policy.

The board needs separate records that are easy to inspect and hard to confuse.
