An audit pack is the collection of documents that proves your board managed finances correctly. Most boards don't have one ready.

What an auditor or attorney typically wants to see when reviewing HOA finances:

- Bank statements for both operating and reserve accounts, confirming they are separate
- Monthly reconciliations showing account balances match records
- The current reserve study, with date and who conducted it
- Percent-funded calculations for each reporting period
- Meeting minutes that document board discussions about reserve funding levels
- Any reserve fund disclosures sent to homeowners
- Budget documents showing reserve contribution amounts

Most self-managed boards can produce some of this. Most cannot produce all of it quickly in a form that's complete and accurate.

The board that has an audit pack ready at all times is in a different position than the board scrambling to reconstruct records after a dispute starts.

Compliance software should generate this documentation automatically, not require a board officer to assemble it from multiple sources the week an audit is requested.

If you cannot pull a complete audit pack for your community in under an hour, that's a systems gap worth addressing.
