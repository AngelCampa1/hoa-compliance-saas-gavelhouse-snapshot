Not yet.

One honest con belongs in the open: Gavelhouse does not have a native mobile app yet. The web interface works on mobile browsers.

That is the straight answer.

For self-managed boards, the first product bet is not a phone icon. It is financial control, governance records, owner operations, reserve deadline tracking, and clean board handoff.

Those are the workflows that decide whether the board can prove what happened and keep funds separated.

Mobile matters. It will matter more as the product matures. But a native app would not fix a reserve account being treated like an operating account, or a study date living in one treasurer's inbox.

We are building the compliance spine first.
