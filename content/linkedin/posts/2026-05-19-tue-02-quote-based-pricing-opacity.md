"Contact us for pricing" is not an answer a volunteer board treasurer can bring to a vote.

CINC Systems starts at $250/month minimum and requires a custom quote. Vantaca is $300 to $500+ per month. Enumerate is also quote-based.

For a professional management company with sales staff, quote-based pricing is manageable. You schedule a call, negotiate a rate, and budget accordingly.

For a volunteer HOA treasurer, the process looks different. You need to bring a specific cost to the board. The board votes on it. If the quote changes, you go back to the board. If the vendor requires a lengthy sales process, you've spent committee hours before knowing whether it's in budget.

Enterprise pricing opaque to the buyer is designed for enterprise buyers. Volunteer boards are not enterprise buyers.

Gavelhouse lists every self-serve Y80OFF plan publicly. Starter is about $10/month billed annually. Growth is about $27/month billed annually. Scale is about $50/month billed annually. Portfolio is custom.

That's a deliberate product choice.
