The first spreadsheet is reasonable.

Then the board adds a tab for reserve components. Another for delinquent dues. Another for owner requests. Another for vendor invoices. Another for board task tracking.

Eventually the spreadsheet is no longer a tool. It is the board's operating system, held together by the person who understands the formulas.

That is risky for a volunteer board.

Gavelhouse exists because self-managed communities need a real operating system: financial control, governance workflows, owner operations, and annual plans from one place.

Spreadsheets can support a board. They should not be the board.
