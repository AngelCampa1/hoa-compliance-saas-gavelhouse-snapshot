Boards often patch the gap this way:

QuickBooks for money. Calendar reminders for reserve study dates. Spreadsheets for owner lists. Email for requests. Meeting minutes in a shared folder.

Each piece can work alone.

The problem is the handoff between them.

When a state deadline, reserve allocation, owner request, and budget decision touch the same issue, the board has to connect the evidence manually.

Gavelhouse is built so the finance and governance pieces live together. Self-managed boards need fewer loose ends, not more tabs to reconcile.
