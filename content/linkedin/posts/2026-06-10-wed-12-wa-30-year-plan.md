Washington requires reserve study updates at least every 3 years, and the study must include a 30-year funding plan.

That long horizon can feel excessive when the board is mostly arguing about this year's dues.

But major components do not care about annual meeting cycles. Roofs, pavement, and structural items age on their own schedule.

A 30-year plan forces the board to connect today's assessment level to tomorrow's replacement cost.

That is uncomfortable. It is also the point of reserve planning.
