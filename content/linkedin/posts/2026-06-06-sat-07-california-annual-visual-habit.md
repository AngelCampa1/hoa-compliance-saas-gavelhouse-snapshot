California's reserve study cycle includes a full study every 3 years and annual visual inspection.

That matters operationally.

A board cannot treat the reserve study as a binder that comes out only when the professional arrives. The annual review is where reality catches up: roofs age, paving conditions change, project timing moves, and the percent-funded story needs to be disclosed.

Good board operations make that review easy to find and easy to repeat.

If the only copy lives in an old attachment, the process is already too fragile.
