QuickBooks is fine software for many small businesses.

The HOA problem is different. Reserve money and operating money have legal meaning. In states like California, Florida, Maryland, New Jersey, and Washington, Gavelhouse identifies explicit separation rules or commingling prohibitions.

Categories inside one accounting habit are not the same as board-level fund controls.

If a volunteer treasurer can accidentally spend reserve cash like operating cash, the system is leaning too hard on memory and discipline.

That is a fragile control for a board that changes officers every year or two.
