A self-managed HOA can be run by volunteers and still need professional-grade records.

That is the hard part.

The board may meet around a kitchen table. The treasurer may be a neighbor doing this after work. But the obligations are still real: reserve tracking, operating fund separation, owner records, meeting decisions, budgets, disclosures, and handoffs.

The risk is not that volunteers are careless. The risk is that the system around them is too loose.

Gavelhouse is for self-managed boards that want the structure without hiring a full management company.
