Per-unit pricing can make software approval harder than it needs to be.

The board has to calculate the current cost, forecast what changes if unit counts shift, and explain why a tool got more expensive as the community grew.

Gavelhouse avoids that.

Gavelhouse tracks self-serve annual plans from Starter through Scale and says there are no per-unit fees on those plans. Portfolio is custom for larger or multi-community operators. For self-managed boards, that matters because pricing has to survive a board meeting.

The goal is not clever packaging.

It is a price the board can understand before voting.
