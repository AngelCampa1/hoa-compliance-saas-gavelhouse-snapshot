The requirement says Fannie Mae is raising its reserve floor to 15% on January 4, 2027 for Full Review loan applications.

That does not mean every association should treat 15% as the whole plan.

State law, the reserve study, component age, funding plan, and upcoming projects still matter. The Fannie Mae number is one requirement in a larger reserve conversation.

Boards should avoid the false comfort of meeting one threshold while ignoring the rest of the record.

Compliance is not the same as good planning.
