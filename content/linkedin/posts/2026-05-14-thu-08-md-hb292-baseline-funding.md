Maryland HB 292 (2025) established mandatory reserve funding. Reserves are no longer a discretionary line in the annual budget.

HB 292 (2025, Chapter 519, effective October 1, 2025) and HB 107 (2022, Chapter 664) together build out Maryland's reserve funding framework. The reserve provisions for HOAs sit in Md. Real Prop. §11B-112.2 (annual budget reserves) and §11B-112.3 (reserve study requirements). HB 292 requires associations to adopt a funding plan in consultation with the reserve study author and to deposit reserve contributions matching the most recent reserve study's recommended amount by the end of each fiscal year.

Maryland's legislative trajectory has been toward stricter requirements. HB 107 in 2022 mandated reserve studies. HB 292 in 2025 added the mandatory funding plan. The direction is consistently toward more obligation, not less.

For Maryland boards currently managing reserves informally, the combined effect of the reserve study mandate, the funding plan requirement, and the budget alignment requirement creates concrete, auditable obligations. Each is separately reviewable.

Enforcement runs through the Maryland Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division under the Maryland Condominium Act and Homeowners Association Act. The time to align the budget with the funding plan is before an audit or complaint surfaces the gap.
