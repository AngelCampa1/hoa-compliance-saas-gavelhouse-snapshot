Starting January 4, 2027, your lender will ask about reserve status before approving a conventional loan in your community.

This is the practical consequence of Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03. The 15% reserve floor is not just a number your accountant tracks. It's a number that affects whether buyers can get a mortgage in your community.

A unit goes on the market. The buyer applies for a conventional loan. The lender requests a condo questionnaire. One of the questions is reserve funding status. If the community is below 15% funded, the underwriter flags the loan.

This creates a chilling effect on property values in underfunded communities. It's not the board's intention. It's a side effect of years of underfunded reserve contributions.
