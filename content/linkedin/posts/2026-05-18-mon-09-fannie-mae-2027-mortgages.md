January 4, 2027.

That's the effective date of Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03. Starting that date, any community applying for a Full Review loan must show at least 15% of annual assessments going to reserves.

This applies to all associations regardless of state law. A community in a state with no mandatory reserve funding requirement is still subject to this rule if their buyers need conventional financing.

What happens when your community doesn't meet the threshold?

The homeowner's mortgage application gets flagged. Lenders can't sell non-conforming loans into the secondary market. Buyers either can't get financing or face much higher rates. Property values in your community become harder to sustain.

The board didn't intend to block mortgages. They just never funded reserves adequately. The outcome is the same.

Less than a year away. Boards that don't have a reserve study showing 15%+ allocation need to start that conversation now.
