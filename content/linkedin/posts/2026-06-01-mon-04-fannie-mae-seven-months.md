Today is June 1, 2026. Lender Letter LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027.

Seven months.

The requirement: for a Full Review loan application on a condo or HOA unit, the association must be allocating at least 15% of its total budgeted income to reserves. This applies to every association in every state, regardless of what local law says.

Here is what June looks like for a board that is ahead of this:

**Check your current allocation ratio.** Take your reserve line item in the current budget. Divide it by total budgeted income. If that number is below 0.15, you are at risk.

**Look at your fiscal year.** If your year runs January to December, your 2027 budget is approved in the fall of 2026. That means this fall's budget cycle is the last opportunity to get above the floor before the rule is live.

**Talk to your lender contacts.** Real estate agents who sell units in your community and mortgage brokers who work your market will feel this change. Boards who are already compliant will have a story to tell.

**Get documentation in order.** Lenders doing Full Reviews will need to see the reserve allocation in the association's approved budget. A clean budget packet matters.

Seven months is enough time. But it is not a lot of time if your fiscal calendar means the budget needs to pass in September.
