Most reserve arguments start too late.

The board is already in budget season. Owners are already reacting to assessment changes. The treasurer is trying to explain why roof money cannot be treated like extra cash.

Start earlier.

Put the reserve components in front of the board before the budget draft: roofs, paving, elevators, gates, fire safety equipment, major building systems. Then show the next required study date, the current balance, and the funding path.

The conversation changes when everyone can see what the money is tied to.

That is the operating habit Gavelhouse is built around: reserve and operating fund separation first, then board decisions.
