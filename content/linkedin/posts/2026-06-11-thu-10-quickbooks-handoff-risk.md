QuickBooks can look fine while the same person runs it for years.

The test comes during the handoff.

Can the next treasurer identify operating funds, reserve funds, restricted cash, approved reserve allocations, and pending capital work without calling the outgoing officer three times?

If the answer depends on local habits, category naming, or a spreadsheet outside the system, the board does not have a durable control.

Self-managed boards need records that make sense after turnover. That is the normal state of volunteer governance.
