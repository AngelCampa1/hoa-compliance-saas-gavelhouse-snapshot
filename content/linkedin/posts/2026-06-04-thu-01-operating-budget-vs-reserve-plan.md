An HOA budget can look balanced and still be weak.

That happens when the operating side gets all the attention. Insurance, landscaping, utilities, management tasks, collections, and ordinary repairs get funded because the bills are visible.

Reserve obligations are quieter until they are not.

The roof ages. Pavement deteriorates. Structural components move closer to replacement. Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve floor gets closer. State disclosures still need the reserve balance, percent-funded status, or funding plan where required.

The board should be able to look at the budget and see both stories at once.

Can we pay this year's bills?

Are we funding the future obligations the reserve study already identified?

If the system answers only the first question, the board is flying with half the panel dark.
