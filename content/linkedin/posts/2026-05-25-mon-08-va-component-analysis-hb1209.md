Virginia Property Owners' Association Act §55.1-1826 requires associations to conduct a reserve study at least every five years.

The study must include a component analysis and a funding plan.

HB 1209 (2024) reinforced these requirements. Component analysis means the board must identify each major reserve component, its current condition, estimated remaining useful life, and replacement cost.

The funding plan must then show how the association will accumulate sufficient reserves to cover those replacement costs before each component fails.

Virginia boards that have a reserve study but no component breakdown, or a study that is more than five years old, are not meeting the §55.1-1826 standard as clarified by HB 1209.

The distinction between a reserve study and a component analysis with a funding plan matters. A study that simply states "you need $500,000 in reserves" without itemizing components and showing how you will get there falls short of what Virginia law requires.
