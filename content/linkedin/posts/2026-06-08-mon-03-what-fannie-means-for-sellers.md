Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve floor is easy to treat as a lender checklist item.

Boards should look at it from the owner's side.

The requirement says underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals once LL-2026-03 takes effect on January 4, 2027. That means the association's reserve posture may affect an owner's ability to sell or refinance.

That is a very practical board issue.

Reserve allocation, fund separation, and documentation should be clean before a transaction depends on them.

Nobody wants the first serious review to happen during a closing.
