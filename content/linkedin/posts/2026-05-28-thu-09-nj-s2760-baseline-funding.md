New Jersey N.J.S.A. 46:8B-19 has long prohibited commingling of reserve and operating funds. The reserve balance cannot drop below zero.

S2760 (2024) and S3992 (2025) are adding baseline funding requirements to that framework. New Jersey is in the process of moving from a prohibition state to a mandate state.

The sequence is similar to Maryland's approach: first establish the structural prohibition on commingling, then layer on the minimum funding obligation. Both bills are working through the New Jersey legislature.

New Jersey condo boards that are technically compliant under the current law (separate accounts, balance above zero) may find they are materially non-compliant under the new framework if the reserve balance, while positive, is far below what the funding mandate will require.

The time to prepare is before the mandate passes, not after. An association that has separate accounts but severely underfunded reserves has the structural compliance but not the substantive compliance. Getting to adequate funded status takes years of consistent contributions.

New Jersey boards should calculate their current percent-funded status now, before the baseline funding requirement is enacted, to understand how large the gap is and how long it will take to close.
