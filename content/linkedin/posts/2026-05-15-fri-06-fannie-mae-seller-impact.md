A homeowner in your community wants to sell. Their buyer needs a Fannie Mae loan. Your reserve allocation will determine whether that sale closes.

This is the practical consequence of Lender Letter LL-2026-03, effective January 4, 2027.

The lender orders a Full Review questionnaire for your association. The questionnaire asks for, among other things, the percentage of the budget allocated to reserves. If that percentage falls below 15%, the loan does not meet Fannie Mae eligibility requirements.

The seller does not control the reserve allocation. The board does. But the seller bears the consequence.

That dynamic creates a direct line between board decisions about reserve funding and every homeowner's ability to sell their unit to a buyer using conventional financing. It is not theoretical. It is a specific transaction that will occur multiple times per year in most communities, and starting January 4, 2027, reserve allocation will be on the eligibility checklist.

Communities that have maintained adequate reserves will clear the 15% bar without any change to their budget. Communities that have been deferring reserve funding are the ones that need to act before that date.

Check your current reserve allocation percentage. If it is close to 15% or below, the 2027 budget cycle needs to address it.
