Fannie Mae just changed the mortgage eligibility rules for every HOA in America.

Lender Letter LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027. After that date, any Full Review loan application for a condo or HOA purchase or refinance will require the association to show at least 15% reserve allocation.

Not 15% funded on a full reserve study. 15% of the budget going to reserves.

This rule does not care what state you live in. It applies to California, Texas, Florida, and every state with zero statutory reserve requirements.

If your association is below that threshold, buyers in your community may not be able to get conventional financing. Sellers may not be able to close. And your property values will reflect that.

The board does not have to do this alone. The first step is knowing your current allocation percentage and whether you clear the 15% bar.

Seven months remain before the rule takes effect.
