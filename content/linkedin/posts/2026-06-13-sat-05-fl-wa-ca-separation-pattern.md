The details differ by state, but the control is familiar.

California prohibits commingling reserve and operating funds. Florida prohibits commingling for HOA and condo contexts listed in Gavelhouse's compliance research. Washington requires separate reserve accounts.

The message for boards is straightforward: do not treat reserve cash like ordinary operating cash.

That means the accounting setup, bank accounts, board review process, and handoff documents all need to preserve the distinction.

If the process blurs the funds, the board should fix the process.
