Maryland's reserve requirements in Gavelhouse's compliance research include separate reserve accounts and a $10,000 fine.

That is too much risk to manage with informal habits.

The board should not rely on one treasurer remembering which transfer is allowed, which account is restricted, or when the next review is due.

The system should make separation obvious. The records should show what happened. The board should review the balances on a regular schedule.

Compliance should not be stored in someone's head.
