"We have money in reserves" is a start. It is not an answer.

Reserve health depends on the assets the association must maintain, the age of those components, the funding plan, upcoming projects, and the contribution schedule.

That is one reason reserve planning keeps coming back to studies, percent-funded status, 30-year plans, and state-specific deadlines.

A reserve balance is a snapshot. Reserve planning is the movie.

Boards need both. One tells you what is in the account. The other tells you whether it is enough.
