We built Gavelhouse because the tools available to self-managed HOA boards were either too simple to be useful or too complex to be manageable.

The spreadsheet and the professional management software. There wasn't much in between.

Over 100,000 self-managed HOAs still run on spreadsheets and QuickBooks. QuickBooks is accounting software. It doesn't know what a reserve fund is. It doesn't know that commingling reserve and operating funds is a liability issue in California, Florida, and Washington. It doesn't enforce the separation. It doesn't track state deadlines. It doesn't give homeowners a portal or automate dues reminders. It's a general-purpose accounting tool doing a job it wasn't built for.

The professional management software is designed for management companies running hundreds of communities. The pricing reflects that. So does the onboarding timeline.

The volunteer treasurer in a 70-unit community shouldn't need a six-week implementation project or a per-unit monthly fee to run compliant finances.

Gavelhouse is built for that treasurer. Reserve and operating funds separated at the database level. State deadline tracking. Dues collection with an audit trail. Month-end close with attested balances. Homeowner portal.
