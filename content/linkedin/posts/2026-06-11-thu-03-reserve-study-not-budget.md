A reserve study tells the board what components exist, when they are expected to need work, and how funding should be planned.

The budget is where the board decides how money actually moves this year.

Boards get into trouble when those two things drift apart. The study says one thing. The approved assessment schedule says another. The spreadsheet has a third version.

That is when nobody knows whether the association is funding the plan or merely storing a PDF.

The study has to connect to the budget.
