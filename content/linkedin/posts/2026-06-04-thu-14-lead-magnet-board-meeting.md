A dues conversation without reserve context is incomplete.

The board needs to know the current reserve balance, the study date, the funding plan, any percent-funded disclosure requirement, and whether Fannie Mae's 2027 rule changes the risk picture for owners.

That is a lot to gather from memory.

The 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide was built for that moment. It puts each state's reserve study and funding requirements in one reference, along with statutes, deadlines, penalties, and the Fannie Mae changes taking effect January 4, 2027.

Use it as a starting point before the meeting packet goes out.

Then ask the harder question: does the proposed budget match the reserve obligation?
