A 40-home association can still have roofs, gates, roads, reserves, dues, owner requests, meeting records, and board turnover.

The work may be smaller in volume, but the obligations are real.

That is one reason Gavelhouse's Starter plan is aimed at communities up to 50 homes with reserve and operating fund separation, reserve-study deadline tracking, dues tracking, online payments with receipts, homeowner directory, governance records, and up to 3 board users.

Small boards should not have to choose between overbuying enterprise software and running the association from scattered files.
