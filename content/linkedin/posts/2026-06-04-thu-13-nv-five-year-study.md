Gavelhouse tracks Nevada under mandate states.

It cites NRS section 116.31152 and says the reserve study frequency is every five years.

Five years sounds easy to remember until the board changes twice, the treasurer moves, and the last study PDF is sitting in a folder nobody has opened since the prior annual meeting.

Reserve compliance is not merely about knowing the rule. It is about keeping the date visible long enough for the next board to act on it.

That is one reason deadline tracking belongs in the board operating system.

The community should not depend on one volunteer remembering a statutory clock that runs longer than many board terms.
