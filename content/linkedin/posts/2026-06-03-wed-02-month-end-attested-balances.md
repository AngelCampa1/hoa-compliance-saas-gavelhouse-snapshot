A reconciled bank balance tells the board what the bank says.

It does not, by itself, show that the board reviewed the operating balance, the reserve balance, open dues, pending invoices, and known reserve obligations.

Gavelhouse tracks month-end close with attested balances as a Scale plan feature. That word matters. Attested means a board officer reviewed and confirmed the closing position.

For self-managed communities, this is not paperwork for its own sake. It creates a dated record that the board had financial oversight.

That record matters during audits, owner disputes, treasurer turnover, and budget season.

The board should not have to recreate its financial position from bank exports and memory after the fact.
