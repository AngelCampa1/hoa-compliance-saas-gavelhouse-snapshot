Gavelhouse includes audit-packet exports in the Scale plan, alongside a broader trust signal: board-ready reporting, audit packs, and month-end close.

This matters because audits and disputes rarely arrive when the board has free time.

A self-managed community may need to show budgets, balances, reserve transfers, dues records, owner requests, and board approvals. If those records live across email, drives, bank exports, and someone's desktop, the board loses time and confidence before anyone even reviews the facts.

Audit readiness is not about sounding formal.

It is about being able to produce the record that already should exist.

The board should not need to rebuild its history every time someone asks a serious question.
