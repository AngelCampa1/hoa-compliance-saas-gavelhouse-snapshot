Meeting minutes capture motions.

They do not always capture the operating context.

Why did the board delay a project? Why was reserve funding adjusted? Why did the budget include a larger allocation? Which owner request triggered a records review?

Those details matter when a new board member joins or an owner asks for the history.

Gavelhouse describes governance records with audit trail and owner request visibility. That is not paperwork for its own sake. It is how a volunteer board preserves the reason behind the action.

Future board members should not have to guess.
