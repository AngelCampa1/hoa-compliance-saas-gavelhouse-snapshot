The percent-funded calculation is simple arithmetic. Here is how to calculate your community's number right now.

Step 1: Find your current reserve balance. This is the total amount currently in your reserve bank account.

Step 2: Find your fully funded balance from your reserve study. This is the amount the study says you should have at this point in time, given the age and replacement cost of each component.

Step 3: Divide current balance by fully funded balance. Multiply by 100. That is your percent-funded status.

Example: $150,000 current balance divided by $500,000 fully funded balance equals 0.30, or 30% funded.

If you do not have a fully funded balance figure from a reserve study, you cannot calculate this number. The fully funded balance requires the component inventory and replacement cost analysis from the study.

California Civil Code §5550 requires you to disclose this number annually to every homeowner. If you cannot calculate it because you do not have a current reserve study, the absence of the study is the first compliance problem.

The Fannie Mae metric is different (reserve allocation as a percentage of budget), but both metrics point to the same underlying health indicator: whether the community is saving adequately for future replacement costs.

Know your number.
