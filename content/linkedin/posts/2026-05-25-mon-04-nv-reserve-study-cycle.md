Nevada Revised Statutes §116.31152 requires every common-interest community to conduct a reserve study at least every five years.

The statute does not offer an exemption for small communities, self-managed boards, or associations that have never done a study before.

The five-year cycle is a recurring obligation. A board that completed a reserve study in 2019 is now past the Nevada deadline, and a board that has never commissioned one was never in compliance.

Nevada's HOA regulatory environment is among the more active in the western United States. The Nevada Real Estate Division has jurisdiction over common-interest communities and takes compliance obligations seriously.

For volunteer boards running their Nevada community on spreadsheets, the reserve study deadline is one of the easiest compliance gaps to miss and one of the hardest to explain after the fact.
