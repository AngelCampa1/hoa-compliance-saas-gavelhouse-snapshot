Oregon Revised Statutes §100.175 requires associations to conduct a reserve study every three to five years.

The requirement includes a funding plan. The study tells you what you have. The funding plan tells you how you will get to where you need to be.

Oregon boards that commission a study and stop there are only halfway through the statutory requirement.

A funding plan under ORS §100.175 must specify the schedule of contributions that will allow the association to fund each component before the end of its useful life. That is a documented commitment, not a verbal understanding among board members.

Self-managed Oregon HOAs often treat the reserve study as a professional service they hire once per decade. The Oregon statute does not support that approach. Three to five years is the outer limit, not the recommended interval.

If your Oregon HOA board is past the five-year mark on its last study, or completed a study without a formal funding plan, the gap is already in your record.
