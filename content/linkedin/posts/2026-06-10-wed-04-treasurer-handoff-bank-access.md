The outgoing treasurer may have the cleanest spreadsheet in the county. That still does not make the handoff complete.

The first handoff question is access.

Who are the authorized signers? Which accounts hold operating cash? Which accounts hold reserves? Who can download statements? Who can approve payments? Where are the prior close records?

If the answers live in one volunteer's inbox, the board has an operational risk.

Treasurer transitions are normal. Losing the financial map during a transition should not be.
