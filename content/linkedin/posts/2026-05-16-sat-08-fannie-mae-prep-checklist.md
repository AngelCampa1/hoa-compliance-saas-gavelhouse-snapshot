Four things your board should confirm before January 4, 2027.

Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications. Here is the preparation checklist.

One: Know your current reserve allocation percentage. This is your annual reserve contribution divided by your total operating budget. If you do not know this number, calculating it is the first task.

Two: If your allocation is below 15%, identify the gap and build a budget adjustment plan for the 2027 fiscal year. The adjustment does not have to happen all at once, but it needs to be on a path toward 15% before lenders start ordering Full Reviews.

Three: Update your reserve study if it is more than three years old. The study provides the documentation that supports your reserve allocation number. A study from 2019 does not credibly support a 2027 questionnaire response.

Four: Confirm your reserve funds are in separate accounts. Fannie Mae's questionnaire asks about commingling as well as reserve levels. A community that fails both questions is in a worse position than one that only fails one.

The deadline is fixed. The preparation is within your board's control.
