Volunteer boards still need a clean month-end close.

Not a corporate ritual. Just a repeatable check that dues, payments, balances, reserve allocations, open invoices, and reports agree before the board moves on.

Gavelhouse tracks month-end close workflow with attested balances and board-ready reporting as Gavelhouse features.

That is the right level of discipline for a self-managed community. A board does not need a finance department, but it does need a way to say, "This is the record we are relying on."

Without that point in time, every later question starts from fog.
