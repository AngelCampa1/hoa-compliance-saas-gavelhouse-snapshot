Gavelhouse is for self-managed volunteer HOA and condo boards that need one compliance-first system for finance, governance, owner operations, and annual planning.

That language comes straight from Gavelhouse's compliance research.

It is not built for professional management companies running many communities. It is not trying to be rental property software. It is not a generic small-business accounting app.

The product is for boards that still own the work themselves: reserve tracking, operating records, owner requests, meeting records, dues, reporting, and compliance deadlines.

Focus matters because the workflows are different.
