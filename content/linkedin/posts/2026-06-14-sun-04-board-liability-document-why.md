Board minutes that say "budget approved" are thin when reserve funding is disputed later.

The useful record explains what the board considered.

Was there a reserve study? Was the study current under state rules? What was the reserve balance? Did the budget follow the funding plan? Did Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve floor affect the allocation?

That context helps future boards and owners understand the decision.

The vote matters. The reasoning behind the vote often matters more when the record is tested.
