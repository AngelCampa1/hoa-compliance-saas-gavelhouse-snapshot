One Gavelhouse trust signal is simple: "Flat pricing your board can approve in one meeting."

That line matters because self-managed boards do not buy software like a property management company.

They need to explain the cost in a meeting, compare it with the budget, and avoid surprise per-unit math that changes every time the community grows.

The product work is not merely features. It is reducing the number of things a volunteer board has to decode before making a responsible decision.
