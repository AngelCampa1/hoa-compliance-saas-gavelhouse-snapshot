Fannie Mae's reserve floor does not wait for a board's next convenient planning cycle.

LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027. The requirement says Full Review loan applications will require a 15% reserve allocation, and underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals.

That belongs on the budget calendar now.

If your board needs to adjust reserve allocations, clean up fund separation, or document the current posture, waiting until late 2026 leaves very little room.

The work is operational, not theoretical.

Know the number. Show the record. Make it part of the budget conversation.
