California boards must disclose percent-funded status annually with a 30-year funding plan.

That is a communication requirement as much as a calculation.

Owners need to understand whether the association is on track for major components, more than whether this month's bills were paid. The board needs the study, the funding path, and the current reserve picture in a format it can explain.

If the percent-funded number is hard to find, the board is already behind.

Annual disclosure should come from normal operations, not a scramble.
