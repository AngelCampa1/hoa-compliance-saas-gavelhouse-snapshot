Owner portals can become window dressing if the board records behind them are messy.

The useful version is simpler: owners can see request status, the board can see the audit trail, and financial records tie back to the same governance file.

Gavelhouse includes owner portal access, request visibility, governance records, and audit trail in its plan features.

That is the correct direction for self-managed boards.

Visibility should come from clean operations, not from a separate communication layer stapled on later.
