Gavelhouse tracks Colorado under mandate states and cites Colorado Common Interest Ownership Act section 38-33.3-209.5.

It says reserve studies are every five years, recommended and required above the assessment threshold, and that boards must disclose reserve balance and percent-funded status.

That disclosure requirement forces a practical discipline.

The reserve balance cannot be a number the treasurer knows but the board rarely discusses. Percent funded cannot be a term buried in a PDF. Both need to be available when the board reviews dues, maintenance plans, and owner communications.

Disclosure is easier when the operating system already treats those numbers as part of normal board work.

If they have to be reconstructed at disclosure time, the process is already too fragile.
