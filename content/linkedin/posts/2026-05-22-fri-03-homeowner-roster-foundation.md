Every HOA system depends on one thing being accurate: the homeowner roster.

If the roster is wrong, dues go to the wrong address. Violation notices miss the right owner. The owner portal shows stale information. And when the board changes hands, whoever inherits the records starts from a broken foundation.

The roster is not just a contact list. It connects to:

- Dues accounts and payment history
- Owner portal access
- Architectural request records
- Delinquency tracking
- Governance communications

Most boards treat the roster as a side task. The treasurer keeps a spreadsheet, the secretary has a different version, and nobody is sure which one is current.

Getting the roster clean and centralized is the first task we tell every new Gavelhouse community to complete. Import the list, confirm the unit-to-owner mappings, and connect it to dues. Everything else builds on top of that.

It takes one afternoon. The cost of not doing it shows up every month after.
