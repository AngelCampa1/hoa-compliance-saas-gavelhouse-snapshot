Virginia's Property Owners' Association Act requires a reserve study at least every 5 years, with component analysis and a funding plan.

Those words matter.

A component analysis forces the board to name what it is responsible for. A funding plan forces the board to connect those components to money over time.

That is the part many volunteer boards try to skip. They want the answer to be "we have cash."

Cash today is not the same as a plan for the assets the association owns.
