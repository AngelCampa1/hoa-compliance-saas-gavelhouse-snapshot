Colorado Common Interest Ownership Act §38-33.3-209.5 requires every association above the assessment threshold to conduct a reserve study at least every five years.

The study must disclose the reserve balance and percent-funded status to all homeowners.

Most self-managed Colorado HOAs are not meeting this requirement. The board members running those communities are exposed to fiduciary duty claims from the moment they skip the study deadline.

Percent-funded status is not just a number for the report. It tells every homeowner whether the community has enough set aside to cover roofs, pavement, elevators, and pools when they fail, or whether a large special assessment is coming.

If your Colorado HOA is past the five-year mark without a current study, that gap shows up in your reserve disclosures and in buyer due diligence.
