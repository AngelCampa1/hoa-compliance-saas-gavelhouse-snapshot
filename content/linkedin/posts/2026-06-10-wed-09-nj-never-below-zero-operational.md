New Jersey's baseline funding rule says the reserve balance can never drop below zero.

That sounds obvious until a board approves a capital project before checking the reserve schedule, payment timing, and next assessment cycle.

The operational question is not "do we have reserves?" It is "will the reserve balance stay above zero after the approved work, planned contributions, and timing of payments are all considered?"

That question belongs in the board workflow before the vote, not after the invoice arrives.
