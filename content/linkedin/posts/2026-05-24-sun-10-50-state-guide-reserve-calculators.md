The 50-state guide and free reserve tools are the same place. Here is what is available at gavelhouse.app/free/ and when to use each.

**50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide**
When to use it: before a board meeting where reserve funding is on the agenda, when a new treasurer is getting oriented, when a unit sale triggers lender questions about reserve status, or when the board is reviewing the annual budget and reserve contributions.

What it covers: every state's reserve statutes, deadlines, disclosure requirements, penalties, and the Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 changes effective January 4, 2027.

**Free reserve calculators**
When to use them: when you want to run quick numbers on your current funding trajectory, check whether your planned contribution rate gets you to the Fannie Mae 15% floor before 2027, or estimate the monthly contribution increase needed to close a funding gap.

Both are free. No signup required.

We built them because the first step for most self-managed boards is just understanding where they stand. The guide answers the compliance question. The calculators answer the financial question.
