Larger self-managed communities need a tighter financial rhythm.

The compliance research's Scale plan includes general ledger and core financial reports, audit-packet exports, month-end close workflow with attested balances, unlimited board users, and priority support.

That is the right direction for communities from 201 to 500 homes.

At that size, small record gaps become bigger board problems. More owners, more payments, more requests, more vendors, more reserve exposure.

The board needs a close process it can repeat and explain, not a treasurer-only file that everyone else trusts because they have to.
