The board can have clean transaction records and still lack a reserve policy.

The policy question is bigger: which account holds reserves, when transfers happen, who reviews balances, how the budget ties to the reserve study, and what documentation owners can see.

QuickBooks can store transactions. It does not decide whether the board is following California's commingling rule, Florida's SIRS funding reality, Washington's separate account requirement, or Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve allocation floor.

Software records the work. The board still needs the control.
