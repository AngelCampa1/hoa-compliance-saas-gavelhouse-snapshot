Gavelhouse tracks Nevada's reserve study frequency as every 5 years under NRS 116.31152.

That date should not live in an old PDF filename.

Boards need the last study date, next required update date, current reserve balance, and budgeted reserve allocation where they can review them together.

The failure mode is predictable: everyone assumes someone else is watching the deadline until budget season arrives and the study is stale.

Reserve compliance gets easier when deadlines are operational, not archival.
