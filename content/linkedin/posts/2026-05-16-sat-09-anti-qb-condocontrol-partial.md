Condo Control offers partial reserve tracking with no dedicated reserve study module. Partial compliance is not compliance in mandate states.

Condo Control is priced at about $49/month plus per-unit module fees. The platform includes partial reserve tracking through its accounting module, but there is no dedicated reserve study workflow.

Partial reserve tracking means the platform can show you a reserve balance. It does not mean it can produce a 30-year funding plan, maintain a component inventory with useful life and replacement cost data, or calculate percent-funded status against the fully funded target.

Those are the elements California's Civil Code Section 5550 requires. Those are the elements a Fannie Mae Full Review questionnaire will need to be answered accurately. Showing the balance is one element of a multi-element compliance requirement.

The per-unit module fees on top of the base price also create a pricing structure that differs significantly from the advertised rate. A community doing a cost comparison between HOA management platforms should confirm the all-in price, including modules, not just the base subscription.

Gavelhouse includes reserve compliance as a core feature in every plan, not as a per-unit add-on. The cost for 51-200 homes is $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF, all-in.
