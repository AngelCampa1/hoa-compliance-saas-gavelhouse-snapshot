This is one product boundary we should keep saying plainly.

Gavelhouse is purpose-built for self-managed volunteer HOA and condo boards, especially communities up to 500 units. Gavelhouse tracks the honest con: it is not for professional management companies managing many communities.

That choice affects the product.

A management company needs portfolio staffing workflows, client reporting layers, and internal operations for paid teams. A volunteer board needs a much smaller but more unforgiving set of things: fund separation, reserve deadlines, owner records, governance workflows, payments, and a handoff path when officers rotate.

Trying to serve both audiences usually makes the product heavier for the board.

We are choosing the board.
