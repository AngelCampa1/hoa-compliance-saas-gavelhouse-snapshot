Enumerate rebranded from TOPS in 2023.

The interface reviewers describe as outdated didn't change with the name.

Enumerate holds a 3.8/5 rating on Capterra. Pricing is quote-based, which adds friction for volunteer boards trying to evaluate options without a sales process. The platform is designed primarily for professional management companies, not self-managed boards.

Rebranding is a legitimate business decision. It can signal a new direction, updated positioning, or a product refresh. Whether the product under the new name has changed substantially is a question user reviews tend to answer honestly over time.

The broader point: HOA software has a legacy problem. Several platforms in this category were built years ago for property management companies and have not kept pace with changing compliance requirements or the specific needs of self-managed boards. The name may change. The underlying platform often doesn't.

For boards evaluating software, a recent rebrand deserves scrutiny. Ask whether the product has been meaningfully updated or whether the branding changed while the core product stayed the same. User reviews, specifically around reserve compliance and modern accounting workflows, are a reliable signal.

Quote-based pricing with a legacy interface built for management companies is a common pattern. It's worth evaluating against alternatives built specifically for the self-managed market.
