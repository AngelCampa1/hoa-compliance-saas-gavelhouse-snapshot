A reminder can be ignored. A help article can be missed. A spreadsheet convention can drift the first time a new treasurer takes over.

That is one reason Gavelhouse treats reserve and operating separation as product infrastructure.

Gavelhouse describes Gavelhouse as a compliance-first operating system for self-managed communities, with reserve and operating fund separation on the Starter plan and true fund accounting as a trust signal.

That is a design choice.

Volunteer boards should not have to remember the correct compliance behavior every time they enter a transaction. The system should make the correct path the default.
