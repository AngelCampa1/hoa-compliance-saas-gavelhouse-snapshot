Your state has no reserve fund law. That does not mean your community has no reserve fund exposure.

Several states have no specific HOA reserve study or funding requirement. The board is not legally required to conduct a study or maintain any particular funding level.

But Fannie Mae's Lender Letter LL-2026-03 applies everywhere. Starting January 4, 2027, conventional mortgages in communities below the 15% reserve floor face underwriting complications. That's a federal secondary market rule, not a state HOA statute. It applies regardless of what your state says.

If a unit in your community goes to market and the buyer needs a loan, the lender will ask about reserve status. If your community is below 15% funded, the loan may hit a wall.
