Fannie Mae is raising the reserve floor to 15% on January 4, 2027 (Lender Letter LL-2026-03).

That change affects every HOA in the country, whether your state has a reserve law or not.

If a unit in your community goes up for sale and the buyer needs a conventional loan, the lender will check your reserve funding status. If you're below 15%, the loan may not close.

We put together the "50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide" to give boards a clear reference for what their state requires and what Fannie Mae requires on top of that. It covers:

- Every state's reserve study and funding statutes
- State-specific deadlines and penalties
- The Fannie Mae 2027 changes in plain language

You don't need to hire a compliance consultant to get a first read on where your community stands. That's what the guide is for.
