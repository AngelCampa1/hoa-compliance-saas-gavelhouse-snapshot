Gavelhouse tracks Utah's Community Association Act requirement: reserve study at least every 6 years, with reserve balance disclosure.

That timeline is long enough for boards to forget it and short enough to matter during normal community life.

A six-year requirement can span several treasurers and board presidents.

So the date cannot live only in the old officer's files. It belongs in the board's operating system, budget calendar, and handoff checklist.

Deadline memory should not reset when volunteers rotate.
