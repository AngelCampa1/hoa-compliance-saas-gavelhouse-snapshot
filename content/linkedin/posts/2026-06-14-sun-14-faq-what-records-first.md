Start with the financial control file.

Find the operating account record, reserve account record, current budget, last reserve study, next study deadline, recent close records, and any owner-facing reserve disclosure.

Then check whether those records agree with each other.

That is the fastest way to find the real problem. Sometimes the issue is missing documentation. Sometimes the reserve study is stale. Sometimes the budget does not match the funding plan. Sometimes the board cannot prove fund separation.

Do the boring inventory first. It tells you where to spend attention.
