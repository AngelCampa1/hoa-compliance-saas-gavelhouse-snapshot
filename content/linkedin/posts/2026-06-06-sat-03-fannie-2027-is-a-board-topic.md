Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027.

The reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications moves to 15%, and Gavelhouse's compliance research is blunt about the impact: underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals.

That makes this a board issue, more than a mortgage issue.

If an owner wants to sell or refinance, the association's reserve posture can end up in the file. A board that treats reserves as a year-end leftover may be creating problems for owners later.

The practical move now is simple: know your reserve allocation, document it, and stop mixing operating cash with long-term repair money.
