Owner questions become painful when the board has to reconstruct basic facts.

When was the last reserve study? What did it recommend? What is the reserve allocation in the current budget? Which account holds the reserve balance? What did the board approve at the last meeting?

Those should be ordinary records, not a special project.

Gavelhouse tracks owner portal visibility, governance records, and audit trails as Gavelhouse plan features.

That is the direction self-managed boards need to move: fewer mysteries, cleaner records, less scrambling.
