Reserve status should not be buried.

Put one page in the board packet that answers the basic questions: current reserve balance, operating balance, next study deadline, required funding path, projects approved but not started, and any owner-facing disclosures that are coming due.

That page does not replace the reserve study. It keeps the study alive in board operations.

Boards make better decisions when reserves are visible at the meeting, not discovered after the meeting.

Gavelhouse is built around that habit: financial control, governance workflows, owner operations, and annual planning in one system.
