How will a lender actually verify your reserve allocation starting January 4, 2027?

Under Fannie Mae's Lender Letter LL-2026-03, the verification happens through the HOA certification or questionnaire process already standard in most conventional loan transactions.

When a buyer applies for a Full Review loan, the lender sends a questionnaire to the HOA or its management contact. The questionnaire asks about the budget, reserve balances, pending litigation, delinquency rates, and other factors.

After January 4, 2027, that questionnaire will include a check for whether at least 15% of annual assessments are allocated to reserves.

If the HOA's most recent approved budget shows less than 15%, the lender has grounds to decline the Full Review or escalate for additional analysis.

For self-managed boards without a professional management company handling questionnaire responses, this process has always been informal. Someone fills in a form based on whatever financial information is available.

If that financial information is not organized, the questionnaire may be answered incorrectly or incompletely. An incorrect answer exposes the board to liability. A missing answer can stall the transaction.

Boards should know what their questionnaire would show today. If the answer is not readily accessible from your financial records, that is a systems problem worth solving before January.
