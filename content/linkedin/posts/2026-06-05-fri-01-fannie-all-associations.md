The requirement says the Fannie Mae rule applies to all associations regardless of state law.

That is the line boards should pay attention to.

State law may be strict, light, disclosure-only, or silent. Fannie Mae's Full Review requirement is its own track: 15% reserve allocation beginning January 4, 2027, under Lender Letter LL-2026-03.

So the board needs a reserve view that works across both questions.

Are we meeting state requirements?

Are we positioned for mortgage review under the 2027 rule?

Those are separate questions with overlapping financial data. If the board cannot answer both from the current budget and reserve plan, it is time to clean up the records before the deadline becomes urgent.
