An HOA can label transactions well and still have weak fund control.

The control question is whether reserve money can be accidentally treated like operating money. In states with explicit commingling prohibitions or separate account requirements, that distinction matters.

Categories help explain transactions after the fact. Controls prevent the wrong thing from happening in the first place.

That is the gap many boards run into with generic accounting tools.

The board needs a system that respects the legal difference between funds, more than a tidy naming convention.
