Fannie Mae Full Review is the questionnaire your association fills out when a homeowner tries to sell.

When a buyer applies for a conforming mortgage on a condo or planned unit development, the lender may order a Full Review of the association. That review includes a questionnaire sent to the HOA or condo board asking about reserve funding levels, pending litigation, deferred maintenance, and other financial health indicators.

Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review applications. Effective January 4, 2027, associations that cannot confirm their reserve allocation meets the 15% threshold will affect loan eligibility.

The questionnaire goes to the board. The board answers it. If the reserve allocation answer falls below 15%, the lender's guidelines may not allow the loan to proceed on standard conforming terms.

The homeowner trying to sell may not know their association is underfunded until the Full Review comes back. At that point, the sale is either renegotiated, the financing changes to a non-conforming product, or it falls through.

Your board should know your reserve allocation percentage today, not when the questionnaire arrives.
