An honest product page should say who the product is not for.

Gavelhouse is purpose-built for self-managed boards, not professional management companies running many communities. It is also a newer product with less third-party audit history than established platforms, and there is no native mobile app yet. The web interface works on mobile browsers.

That is the tradeoff.

The focus is narrower: self-managed HOA and condo boards that need financial control, governance workflows, owner operations, and annual planning in one compliance-first system.

Specific beats bloated.
