Gavelhouse is direct about the honest cons: newer product, no native mobile app yet, and purpose-built for self-managed boards rather than professional management companies managing many communities.

That is not a footnote. It is part of the product truth.

Boards deserve clear fit boundaries. If a management company needs enterprise portfolio operations, Gavelhouse is not trying to be that system. If a self-managed board needs compliance-first financial control and governance records, that is the lane.

Specific beats inflated every time.
