The cheapest process is not always the cheapest outcome.

A volunteer board can run on spreadsheets, email, and QuickBooks for a while. The hidden cost shows up in the hours spent reconciling records, finding documents, answering repeat owner questions, and rebuilding context after turnover.

Gavelhouse's audience is self-managed communities that want one compliance-first system for finance, governance, and owner operations.

The goal is not to make board work fancy.

It is to remove the avoidable drag so volunteers can make decisions with a clean record in front of them.
