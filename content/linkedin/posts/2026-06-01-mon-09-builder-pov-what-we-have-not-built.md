I am going to write the post I rarely see from founders.

Here is what Gavelhouse does not have:

**A native mobile app.** The web interface works on mobile browsers, and it is reasonably functional there. But it is not a native iOS or Android app. If you want a dedicated app you can tap in from your phone, that is not Gavelhouse today.

**A long audit history.** We are a newer product. Established platforms have years of third-party audits, customer references, and community track records. We do not have that yet. If that matters to your board's procurement decision, I understand it.

**Support for professional management companies.** Gavelhouse is built for self-managed volunteer boards, up to 500 units. If you are a professional manager running 30 communities, the product is not designed for that workflow.

Why say this?

Because boards making a software decision deserve an accurate picture of what they are buying. "Compliance-first" is a real design commitment in Gavelhouse. It is also not the same as "complete product."

I would rather a board understand what we do not have, choose us anyway because the compliance infrastructure matters to them, and have a good experience than oversell capabilities we have not built and start the relationship with a gap.

That is the kind of company I want to build.
