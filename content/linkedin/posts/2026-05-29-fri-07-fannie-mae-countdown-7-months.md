7 months until Fannie Mae's reserve rule takes effect.

January 4, 2027 is the effective date for Lender Letter LL-2026-03. After that date, Full Review loan applications for homes in your community will require at least 15% reserve allocation.

Here is the three-step check your board should run right now.

Step 1: Pull your current approved budget. Find the total assessment income line and the reserve contribution line.

Step 2: Divide reserve contributions by total assessments. If that number is less than 0.15, you are below the Fannie Mae floor.

Step 3: If you are below 15%, calculate what dues increase or special assessment would close the gap before your next budget cycle.

Seven months sounds like a long runway. But most communities set their annual budget in Q4. If you need a dues increase to hit 15%, the board vote needs to happen before the budget is finalized, and homeowners typically get 30 to 60 days notice before a dues change takes effect.

The timeline is tighter than it looks.
