The requirement says the Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 requirement applies to all associations regardless of state law.

That line matters.

A board cannot answer a lender concern by saying its state has no formal reserve study mandate. The Full Review requirement is a separate financing standard with a 15% reserve allocation floor starting January 4, 2027.

State law still matters. The reserve study still matters. The funding plan still matters.

But none of those erase the Fannie Mae review.
