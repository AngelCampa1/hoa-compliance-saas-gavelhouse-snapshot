A growing community asked: "What happens to our data if we outgrow the Starter tier?"

The answer is straightforward: move to the tier that matches your community size. The record stays intact.

There is no data migration, no export-and-reimport, no service interruption. You move from Starter (up to 50 homes) to Growth (51 to 200 homes) when your community adds units that push you over the threshold.

This matters more than it might seem at first.

Financial continuity is a compliance concern. State statutes in California, Florida, New Jersey, and others require HOAs to maintain accurate reserve fund records over years, sometimes decades. A software platform that forces a data migration when you scale creates a break in that record chain.

If a lender questionnaire or state audit asks for reserve contribution history going back five years, and your records show a gap because you switched platforms or converted a file format, that is a documentation problem.

Gavelhouse keeps the record intact as the community grows. The history you build in year one is still there in year four.

Growth should not cost you your compliance history.
