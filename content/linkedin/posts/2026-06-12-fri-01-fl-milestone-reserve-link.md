The compliance research notes Florida milestone inspections at 30 years, or 25 years near the coast, plus SIRS every 10 years for condos and co-ops three stories or taller.

Boards sometimes treat inspection and funding as separate lanes.

They should not.

An inspection can expose building needs. The reserve study and budget decide whether the association has a plan to pay for them. If those records live in different folders, the board is making its own job harder.

Structural oversight and reserve funding have to meet at the board table.
