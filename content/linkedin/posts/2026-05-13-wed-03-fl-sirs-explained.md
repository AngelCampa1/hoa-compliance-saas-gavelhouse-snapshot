Florida condos 3+ stories must complete a Structural Integrity Reserve Study every 10 years.

That requirement came from SB 4-D (2022), the first major Surfside response. HB 913 (2025) extended and tightened those requirements further.

The SIRS covers specific structural components. It is not the same as a standard reserve study, which covers all major common elements. The SIRS focus is the building envelope: roof, structural systems, balconies, and load-bearing components.

Milestone inspections add a separate layer. Buildings 30 years old or older (25 years if within three miles of the coast) require a structural inspection. The inspection then informs whether a Phase 2 engineering review is needed.

Both the SIRS requirement and the milestone inspection create documentation obligations. The study and inspection reports must be maintained and, for the SIRS, the funding plan must be adopted.

Florida Chapter 718 (condos) and Chapter 720 (HOAs) together make Florida the most comprehensive post-Surfside reform state. If your Florida condo board has not confirmed compliance with both the SIRS and the milestone inspection timeline, that is the starting point.
