California SB 410 (2025) continued tightening the Davis-Stirling framework. If your California board has not reviewed compliance since 2024, the requirements have changed.

SB 900 (2024), AB 2114 (2024), and SB 410 (2025) are three separate pieces of legislation that have updated California HOA and condo financial requirements in a compressed two-year window.

The baseline Davis-Stirling requirements under Civil Code §5550-5560 were already among the most comprehensive in the country: reserve studies every three years, annual visual inspections, percent-funded disclosure, and a 30-year funding plan. The 2024-2025 legislation added further obligations on top of that foundation.

For California boards, "we did our reserve study in 2022 so we are current" may not be accurate. The study may be within the three-year window, but the requirements that study needs to satisfy may have changed since 2022.

California Civ. Code §5510's commingling prohibition and its $100-$500 per day penalty remain unchanged and fully in force.

If your California community has not done a full compliance review under the updated 2025 requirements, the next budget cycle is the right time to do it.
