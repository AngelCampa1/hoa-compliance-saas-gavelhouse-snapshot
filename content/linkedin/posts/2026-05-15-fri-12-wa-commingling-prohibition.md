Washington RCW 64.34.364 requires separate reserve accounts for condos. RCW §64.90 extends this to common interest communities.

Washington is a mandate state for both reserve studies and fund separation. The three-year study update cycle and the separate account requirement work together to create a comprehensive reserve compliance framework.

The separate account requirement means the reserve funds must be held in a legally distinct bank account, not just tracked separately in an accounting system. The account structure is the compliance requirement, not the reporting structure.

Washington's reserve study must include the 30-year funding plan under RCW §64.34.382. The combination of the plan and the separated funds creates the documentation and structural compliance that Washington law requires.

For Washington boards using QuickBooks with a single bank account for both operating and reserve funds, the compliance gap is the account structure. Fixing it requires opening a separate reserve account and restructuring how dues payments are deposited and allocated.

The fix is administrative, not complex. But it needs to be done before an audit or a homeowner challenge brings the structure into focus.
