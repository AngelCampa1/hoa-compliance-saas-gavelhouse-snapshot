Maryland's reserve section in Gavelhouse's compliance research is short, but it should get a board's attention.

The state requires separate reserve accounts under MD Code, Real Prop. §11-109.1, and Gavelhouse tracks $10,000 fines as the highest explicit monetary penalty of any state.

That is the kind of rule a volunteer board should not track from memory.

At minimum, the system should know where the reserve account is, what the current balance is, when reserve work is due, and how that record gets handed to the next treasurer.

Compliance is much easier when the operating model expects it.
