Is your HOA reserve fund actually compliant?

Most boards don't know the answer to that question. Not because they're negligent. Because the requirements vary by state, change with new legislation, and aren't summarized anywhere that's easy to find.

California, Florida, Maryland, New Jersey, and Washington all have different requirements. Twenty other states have requirements of their own. Fannie Mae added a 15% allocation requirement effective January 4, 2027.

Most boards know their community. Fewer know whether their reserve fund meets the statutory requirements of their state. Even fewer know whether they'll meet the Fannie Mae threshold before the deadline.
