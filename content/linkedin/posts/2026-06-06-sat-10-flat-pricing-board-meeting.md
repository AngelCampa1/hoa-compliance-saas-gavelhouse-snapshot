HOA boards do not buy software like a single executive with a company card.

They discuss it. They ask what it costs next year. They ask whether the price changes as homes are added. They compare it against the cost of hiring management.

That is one reason Gavelhouse uses flat annual plans for communities up to 500 units, with no per-unit fees on any plan.

The board should be able to look at the price, know the tier, and make a decision in one meeting.

Complex pricing creates another volunteer project. Boards already have enough of those.
