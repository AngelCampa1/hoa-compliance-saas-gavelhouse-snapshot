Some states require HOAs to disclose reserve fund status to owners annually. Is your state one of them?

California's Civil Code §5550 requires percent-funded status to be disclosed in the annual budget report. Florida boards have financial disclosure requirements tied to the annual meeting. Washington's reserve laws include disclosure provisions that apply to condominiums.

Missing a required disclosure is not just a paperwork oversight. Owners who discover the board failed to disclose required financial information have grounds to challenge board decisions and, in some cases, file complaints with state agencies.

But most boards don't know exactly what their state requires. The laws are dense, the statutes are scattered, and the deadlines vary.
