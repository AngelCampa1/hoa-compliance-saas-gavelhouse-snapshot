Gavelhouse tracks dues tracking and online payments with receipts as Starter plan features, with automated dues reminders and delinquency tracking in Growth.

That belongs in the reserve conversation.

If assessments are late or collections are unclear, the board's cash position gets harder to read. If the cash position is hard to read, reserve transfers and operating decisions become more fragile.

Reserve planning depends on boring collection discipline.

The board needs to know what was billed, what was paid, what is outstanding, and which receipts support the record. Then it can review operating cash and reserve funding without guessing.

This is where finance and owner operations meet.

A cleaner dues workflow makes the reserve conversation less emotional because the board starts from facts.
