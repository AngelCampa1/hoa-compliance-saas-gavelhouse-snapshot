Self-managed boards do not need more ceremony.

They do need a repeatable reserve check.

Once a month, answer the same questions: What is the reserve balance? What is the operating balance? What study deadline is coming up? What projects were approved? What owner-facing disclosures depend on these numbers?

That small habit keeps the board from treating reserves as something to revisit only during budget season.

Gavelhouse is built for that kind of routine: true fund accounting, reserve deadlines, board-ready reporting, and governance records in one place.
