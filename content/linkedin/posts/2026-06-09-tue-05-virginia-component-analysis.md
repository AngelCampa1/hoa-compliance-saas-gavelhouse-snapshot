Virginia requires reserve studies at least every 5 years, including component analysis and a funding plan.

That is the right pairing.

A component list without funding is just an inventory of future problems. A funding plan without component detail is hard for owners to trust.

Boards need both in the same operating view: what the community owns, when major work is expected, what the plan says, and how the budget supports it.

That is the reserve conversation a board can actually govern.
