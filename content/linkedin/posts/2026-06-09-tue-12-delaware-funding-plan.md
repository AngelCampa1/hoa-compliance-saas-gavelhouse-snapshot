Delaware requires reserve studies at least every 5 years, including a funding plan.

That last part is where boards should pay attention.

A study without a funding plan leaves the board with a list of future costs but no operating path. A funding plan turns the study into budget work, dues work, reserve tracking, and owner communication.

The plan has to live where the board makes decisions.

If the funding path is buried in a report nobody opens, the board is not really using it.
