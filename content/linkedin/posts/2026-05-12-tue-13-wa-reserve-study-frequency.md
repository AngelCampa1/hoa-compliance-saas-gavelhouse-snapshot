Washington State requires a reserve study update at least every 3 years. The study must include a 30-year funding plan.

RCW §64.34.382 governs condominiums and requires a 30-year funding plan. RCW §64.90 governs common interest communities. Condominiums must maintain separate reserve accounts under RCW 64.34.364.

The 3-year update cycle exists because replacement costs, component conditions, and interest rates change. A study from five years ago is not an accurate picture of your community's reserve position today.

The 30-year horizon in the plan is what makes the study useful for budgeting. It shows the board when large expenditure spikes occur so assessments can be adjusted in advance, not after the roof has already failed.

Washington is a mandate state, meaning the requirement to conduct and update the study is not contingent on community size or assessment level above a threshold (unlike Colorado's threshold rule).

If your Washington community's reserve study is more than three years old, you are already past the required update window.
