Gavelhouse tracks Pennsylvania as a disclosure state.

It cites the Uniform Planned Community Act, 68 Pa.C.S. section 5304, and says reserve information must be included in the public offering statement.

That is a reminder that reserve records are not merely internal board material.

At some point, reserve information can become buyer-facing, owner-facing, lender-facing, or dispute-facing. If the board treats reserve data as something to assemble only when asked, the association is already behind.

Good operations make the information available before pressure arrives.

That means the reserve balance, study status, funding assumptions, and board decisions should be organized as part of regular work.

Disclosure is much easier when the board has been keeping the record all along.
