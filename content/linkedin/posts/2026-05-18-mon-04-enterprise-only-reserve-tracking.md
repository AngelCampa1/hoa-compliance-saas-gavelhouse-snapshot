Some HOA platforms only include reserve fund tracking on their Enterprise tier.

TownSq is one example: reserve tracking is not available on the base plan. You need the Enterprise tier to access it.

Here's the problem with that structure: reserve fund compliance is not an enterprise feature. It is a legal requirement for every HOA board, including the smallest 30-unit community that can't afford to upgrade to a top-tier plan.

A board running on a basic software tier that lacks reserve tracking is not protected. They have software and liability exposure.

The logic of gating compliance tools behind pricing tiers works well for CRM features or advanced analytics. It does not work for statutory obligations.

Gavelhouse includes reserve compliance on every plan, including Starter at $10/month billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 50 homes. No gating. No upgrade required to stay compliant.

If your current tool requires an upgrade to access reserve tracking, ask your vendor what tier covers your state's specific reserve requirements.
