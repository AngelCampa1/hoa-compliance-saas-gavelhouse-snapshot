Self-managed boards do not usually work on a neat office schedule.

The president reviews agenda items Saturday morning. The treasurer checks dues after dinner. The secretary looks for last year's minutes before a meeting. Someone answers an owner question between errands.

That is one reason the system matters.

If records live across personal drives, email threads, and spreadsheets, weekend board work becomes detective work. Volunteers lose time finding the current version before they can make the decision.

Gavelhouse's point of view is simple: volunteer boards need one place for finance, governance, owner operations, and annual planning.
