Is Gavelhouse for condos or HOAs? Both. The compliance problem is the same structure.

Whether your association is organized as a homeowners association or a condominium association, the statutory obligations around reserve funding and fund separation apply in similar form across most states.

Florida's structural reserve requirements, triggered after the Surfside collapse, apply specifically to condominium associations under FL Stat. §718. California's reserve study requirements and commingling prohibition under CA Civ. Code §5510 and §5300 apply broadly to common interest developments.

The Fannie Mae rule under LL-2026-03, effective January 4, 2027, applies to both attached housing structures. Full Review loan applications trigger the 15% reserve allocation check regardless of whether the entity is an HOA or a condo association.

Gavelhouse is built for self-managed communities in either structure, up to 500 units. The fund separation, reserve tracking, and board-ready reporting work the same way whether the governing documents say "HOA" or "condominium association."

The primary qualifier is self-managed. If your community is run by a volunteer board without a professional management company, Gavelhouse is built for your situation.
