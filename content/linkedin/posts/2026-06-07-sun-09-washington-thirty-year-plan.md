Washington's reserve study requirements include a 30-year funding plan.

That is a very different time horizon from the normal board calendar.

Most volunteer boards think in annual budgets, current dues, this year's vendors, and the next meeting. The reserve study pulls the conversation across decades. Roofs, pavement, exterior systems, and major components do not care who is serving this term.

The board's job is to preserve continuity.

That means the reserve plan cannot live only with the current treasurer. It has to survive board turnover.
