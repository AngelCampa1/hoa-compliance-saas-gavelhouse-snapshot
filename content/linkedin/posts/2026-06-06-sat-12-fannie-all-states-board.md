Some boards think about reserves only through their state statute.

That will not be enough for the Fannie Mae 2027 change.

The requirement says LL-2026-03 applies to all associations regardless of state law. The effective date is January 4, 2027, and the requirement is a 15% reserve allocation for Full Review loan applications.

That means a board in a permissive state still has a reason to clean up reserve tracking before the rule matters to an owner's loan file.

The safe operating move is to know the reserve allocation now, not after a sale gets stuck.
