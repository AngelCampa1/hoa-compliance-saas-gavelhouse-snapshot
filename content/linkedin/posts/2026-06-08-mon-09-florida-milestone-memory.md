Florida's SIRS requirements are not the kind of thing a board should track in someone's calendar only.

The requirement says condos and co-ops three stories or taller need a Structural Integrity Reserve Study every 10 years, with milestone inspections at 30 years and 25 years near the coast.

Those are long timelines.

Board members will change. Treasurers will rotate. Files will move.

The deadline has to survive all of that.

Gavelhouse tracks reserve-study deadlines for state statutes because self-managed boards need continuity across volunteer terms.
