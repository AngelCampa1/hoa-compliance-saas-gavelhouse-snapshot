State law and lending rules need to be tracked separately.

Some states have no specific reserve provisions. Some authorize or encourage reserves without a formal study mandate. Some require disclosures but not a formal study.

Fannie Mae's 2027 rule cuts across that state-by-state patchwork.

For Full Review loan applications, Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation requirement beginning January 4, 2027. The requirement says it applies to all associations regardless of state law.

That means "our state does not require a reserve study" is not the same as "our reserve funding cannot affect owners."

Boards need to understand both tracks: the state compliance track and the mortgage review track.

They are not the same thing.
