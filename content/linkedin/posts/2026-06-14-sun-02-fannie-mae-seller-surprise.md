Reserve underfunding often feels internal until a sale or refinance exposes it.

The requirement says Fannie Mae's LL-2026-03 reserve floor starts January 4, 2027 and that underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals.

That puts board budgeting into the path of an owner's transaction.

Nobody wants to discover the issue when a buyer's lender is already asking questions.

Boards have time to prepare, but only if the reserve allocation, study, and records are reviewed before the deadline arrives.
