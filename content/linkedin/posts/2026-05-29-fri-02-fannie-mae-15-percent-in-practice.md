What does "15% reserve allocation" actually mean for your HOA budget?

Under Fannie Mae's Lender Letter LL-2026-03, effective January 4, 2027, at least 15% of your total assessment income must be directed to your reserve fund.

Here is what that looks like with real numbers.

If your community collects $200,000 per year in dues, $30,000 of that must go to reserves. If you are currently allocating $18,000, you fall short. Homeowners who try to buy or refinance after January 4, 2027 may find lenders declining their Full Review applications because of your shortfall.

This is not a reserve study percent-funded calculation. It is a budget allocation check. A lender will look at your most recent budget or financial statement and divide reserve contributions by total assessments.

Boards that raised dues recently may already be close. Boards that deferred a reserve increase for years may have a larger gap to close.

The calculation is simple. Whether there is political will to raise dues is a different problem entirely.
