Most HOA board members are volunteers. They have jobs, families, and limited time.

The standard still matters.

The compliance research points to fiduciary duty as the primary enforcement mechanism in states without specific reserve provisions. In states with explicit reserve statutes, the board also has concrete study, disclosure, account separation, or funding obligations.

That combination is why reserve work deserves board-level attention.

Good faith is not a record. The board still needs to show what it reviewed and why it acted.
