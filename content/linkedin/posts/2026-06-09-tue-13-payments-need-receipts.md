Taking payments online is only half the job.

The board also needs receipts on record, dues status tied to the right owner, and a way to understand what is overdue without building a manual tracker.

Gavelhouse tracks dues tracking and online payments with receipts in Gavelhouse's Starter plan, then automated dues reminders and delinquency tracking in Growth.

That progression matches how boards actually operate.

First, get the payment record clean. Then reduce the manual follow-up as the community grows.

Financial control starts with boring accuracy.
