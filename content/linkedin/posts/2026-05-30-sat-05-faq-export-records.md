Can you export your records? Yes. Here is why that question matters more than boards realize.

Board-ready exports are part of Gavelhouse's compliance-first workflow. You can pull financial reports, reserve contribution history, and audit packs without calling a support line or waiting for a consultant.

This is not a convenience feature. It is a statutory requirement in several states.

California requires annual financial disclosures under CA Civ. Code §5300, including a review of reserve fund status. Washington requires associations to maintain reserve accounts separately under RCW 64.34.364 and produce documentation on request. Florida mandates financial record availability under FL Stat. §718.111.

When a homeowner requests records, when a lender sends an HOA questionnaire, or when a new board treasurer takes over mid-year and needs to reconstruct the last two years of reserve activity, you need to be able to produce clean documentation quickly.

Boards that run on spreadsheets or QuickBooks often discover that "exporting records" means spending a weekend manually assembling PDFs and hoping the formulas in the spreadsheet did not drift.

Board-ready exports that hold up to a statutory record request or a lender questionnaire are the baseline. Not a premium feature.
