Gavelhouse tracks a group of states with no specific reserve provisions.

That does not mean the board has no reserve responsibility. The requirement says general fiduciary duty applies. It also says the Fannie Mae 2027 reserve floor still applies.

This is where boards can misread silence as permission.

A statute may not tell the board exactly how often to update a reserve study. It may not mandate a funding level. But directors still have to make reasonable decisions for the community, document those decisions, and avoid treating long-term obligations like optional expenses.

Fiduciary duty is less convenient than a checklist because it asks whether the board acted prudently.

Good records help answer that question.
