A 20-home board might remember every request by name.

A 150-home board cannot run that way.

The compliance research's Growth plan adds governance records with full audit trail on owner requests, owner portal request visibility, automated dues reminders, delinquency tracking, and up to 10 board users.

That is not merely more software for a bigger community.

It reflects a real operating shift. As owner volume grows, the board needs process instead of memory. Requests need status. Dues need follow-up. Records need continuity across more volunteers.

Growth makes informal systems fail faster.
