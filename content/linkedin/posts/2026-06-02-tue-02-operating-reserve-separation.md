Operating funds answer this question: can we pay the bills due this month?

Reserve funds answer a different one: will we have money for known future repair and replacement obligations?

Boards get into trouble when those two balances blur. A single healthy bank balance can make the community feel fine while the reserve plan is quietly falling behind.

The product source calls out true fund accounting and no commingling as a Gavelhouse trust signal. That is not decoration. It is the difference between "we have money" and "we have the right money in the right fund for the right obligation."

For self-managed boards, that distinction should be visible before the annual meeting, before the audit packet, and before a homeowner asks why dues are going up.
