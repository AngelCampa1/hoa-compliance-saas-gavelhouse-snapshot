A reserve study can be technically current and still poorly used.

The report may exist. The board may have approved it. The file may sit in a shared folder. But if the budget does not reflect it, the treasurer cannot find it quickly, and the next board does not know the update date, the study is not shaping operations.

That is where self-managed boards often get stuck.

The document is not enough. The board needs a habit: review the study, separate the funds, track the deadline, and connect the budget to the funding path.

That is reserve work after the PDF arrives.
