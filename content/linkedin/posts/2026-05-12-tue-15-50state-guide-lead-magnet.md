Every state has different reserve fund rules. One document covers all of them.

Some states require a reserve study every three years (California, Washington). Some require it every five (Colorado, Nevada). Some mandate it for condos above certain thresholds. Some only require disclosure with no study mandate at all.

Enforcement varies: California imposes daily fines ($100 to $500/day under Civ. Code §5510); Florida's DBPR can fine condo and co-op boards up to $5,000 per violation under §718.501(1)(d)(6); Maryland enforces its mandatory reserve funding (HB 292, 2025) through the Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division.

And then there is the Fannie Mae change that applies to all of them. Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loans, effective January 4, 2027. That deadline does not check which state your community is in.
