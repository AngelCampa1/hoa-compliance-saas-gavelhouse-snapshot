TownSq's reserve tracking is an Enterprise-tier feature. The plan most boards buy does not include it.

TownSq is priced at $90/month for communities up to 300 units. The financial module lacks reserve tracking except on the Enterprise tier.

That pricing structure means a board purchasing the standard $90/month plan gets community management features but not the reserve compliance functionality that mandate-state statutes require. Then, when the board discovers the gap, the path to adequate reserve tracking requires an upgrade to a more expensive tier.

The upgrade model is a reasonable business decision for the vendor. It is a compliance risk for the board, because the gap between "what we are paying for" and "what state law requires" can exist without anyone on the board noticing until an audit.

For communities in California, Florida, Maryland, Washington, Nevada, and Colorado, reserve tracking is not an optional upgrade. It is a statutory requirement.

Gavelhouse includes reserve tracking in every plan, starting at $10/month billed annually with Y80OFF. The fund separation and reserve compliance functionality is not tiered behind a more expensive subscription. It is the product.
