Washington's reserve rules are not limited to study timing.

Washington law requires separate reserve accounts under RCW 64.34.364. Boards also need reserve study updates at least every 3 years and a 30-year funding plan.

That combination matters.

The study tells the board what future obligations look like. The separate account structure protects the money assigned to those obligations.

Boards should treat those as connected controls, not independent checklist items.
