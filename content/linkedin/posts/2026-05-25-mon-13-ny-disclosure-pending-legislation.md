New York Real Property Law and General Business Law require reserve fund information to be disclosed in offering plans and in annual reports to homeowners.

That disclosure obligation has been in place for years. The question is whether the disclosures being made are accurate and whether the boards producing them have current data to support them.

Pending legislation S7600 and A8945 would strengthen the reserve requirement beyond disclosure. Both bills are working through the legislature.

If S7600 or A8945 passes, New York would move from a disclosure state to a mandate state with an actual study requirement. The transition would catch boards that have been making disclosures without conducting formal reserve analyses.

New York boards in condo and HOA communities should not wait for the legislation to pass before getting a current reserve study. The offering plan disclosure obligation exists now. A study conducted now gives the board accurate data for that disclosure and positions the community ahead of any legislative change.
