Here is a simple test for a self-managed board:

If the treasurer resigned tonight, could another board member find the current reserve study, the next required update date, bank account structure, dues status, open owner requests, and last close packet by Monday morning?

If not, the board has a continuity problem.

This is not about blaming the treasurer. Most volunteers inherit a messy setup and do their best.

The fix is to stop treating board knowledge as personal knowledge.

The system should carry the record so the people can rotate.
