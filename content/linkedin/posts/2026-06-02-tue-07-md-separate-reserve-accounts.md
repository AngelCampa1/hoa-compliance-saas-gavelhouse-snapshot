Gavelhouse tracks Maryland under mandate states with a direct separate-account requirement.

It cites Maryland Code, Real Property section 11-109.1 for separate reserve accounts, Maryland Homeowners Association Act section 11B-112, and a $10,000 fine as the highest explicit monetary penalty in the state data.

That is not the kind of rule a board wants living in someone's notes.

Maryland also appears with mandatory baseline funding under HB 292 (2025). The operating takeaway is simple: reserve money needs its own treatment, its own records, and its own board visibility.

A volunteer board can still run lean. It cannot run loose.

The more serious the penalty, the less sense it makes to manage reserve separation through memory and spreadsheet discipline.
