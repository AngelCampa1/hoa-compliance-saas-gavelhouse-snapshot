Some communities need a professional management company.

Gavelhouse is not pretending otherwise.

The product is built for self-managed boards that want to keep control but need stronger financial, governance, owner operations, and planning systems. That is a specific audience, and the requirement says the product is not for professional management companies managing many communities.

That clarity matters.

If your board wants full-service staffing, software alone will not do that job. If your board wants to stay self-managed without running on scattered tools, that is the problem Gavelhouse is built to solve.
