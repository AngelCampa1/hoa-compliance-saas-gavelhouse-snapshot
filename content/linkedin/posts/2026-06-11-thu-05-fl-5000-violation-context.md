Gavelhouse tracks Florida's commingling prohibition for HOA and condo reserve and operating funds, plus a $5,000 per violation penalty.

That number should move the conversation out of the "we have always done it this way" category.

Reserve money needs its own treatment. Operating cash needs its own treatment. The board should be able to show the difference without decoding a treasurer's personal spreadsheet.

Compliance work feels expensive until the board is explaining a preventable fund mistake.
