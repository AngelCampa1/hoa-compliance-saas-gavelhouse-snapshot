No.

Gavelhouse is not a licensed professional, and the product should not be described that way.

Gavelhouse positions Gavelhouse as a compliance-first HOA board operating system. That means the software helps self-managed boards keep financial control, governance workflows, owner operations, and reserve deadlines in one place.

It does not replace a reserve specialist who prepares a study. It does not replace a CPA. It does not replace legal advice.

The product's job is narrower and more practical: keep the board's records, workflows, and fund controls organized enough that professional advice can be acted on instead of lost in email.

That boundary matters.

Software should support board responsibility. It should not pretend to erase it.
