Boards ask us: "Is pricing per door?" No. Here is why that matters.

Per-unit pricing sounds simple until you run the math on a 200-unit community. At $2 to $4 per door per month, that is $400 to $800 per month for software, before any implementation fees, training costs, or module add-ons.

Gavelhouse's Y80OFF pricing is flat per community size, billed annually. Starter is $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 50 homes. Growth is $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for 51 to 200 homes. Scale is $50/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for 201 to 500 homes. Portfolio is custom pricing for larger or multi-community operators.

For a 200-unit community on the Growth plan, that is about 20 cents per door per month. The math works in front of a homeowner board in a way that per-unit pricing often does not.

Volunteer boards are spending other people's money. Flat pricing that fits a line item without requiring a spreadsheet justification is easier to approve in a single meeting.

There are no per-unit fees. No setup fee. Month-to-month if you prefer. Annual billing lowers the effective monthly rate.

Is your current management software charging per door? What does that number look like annually?
