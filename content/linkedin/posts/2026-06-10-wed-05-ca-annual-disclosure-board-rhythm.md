California boards cannot commission a reserve study and put it on a shelf.

The compliance research tracks the Davis-Stirling requirements this way: visual inspection annually, full study every 3 years, annual disclosure of percent-funded status, and a 30-year funding plan.

That is a rhythm.

It also means the board needs records that survive officer turnover. Last year's disclosure, the current funding plan, the inspection status, and the next study date should be easy to find.

If finding them takes a search party, the process is too fragile.
