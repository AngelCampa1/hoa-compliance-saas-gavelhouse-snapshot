What size community is Gavelhouse built for? Self-managed, up to 500 units.

From small townhome HOAs with 20 homes to larger condo associations with 400 units, the compliance obligations are similar and the operational gap is the same: volunteer boards managing real financial and legal requirements without professional management infrastructure.

The difference between a 20-unit community and a 400-unit community is mostly scale, not complexity. Both need fund separation. Both need reserve tracking. Both will face lender questionnaires once Fannie Mae's LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027 and lenders start checking 15% reserve allocation on Full Review applications.

The Starter plan covers communities up to 50 homes at $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF. Growth covers 51 to 200 homes at $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF. Scale covers 201 to 500 homes at $50/mo billed annually with Y80OFF.

For communities above 500 homes or boards managing multiple communities, Portfolio is custom.

All flat pricing. No per-unit fees. The budget math works at any size.

If you are not sure which tier fits your community, the unit count determines it. You can always move up if you grow.
