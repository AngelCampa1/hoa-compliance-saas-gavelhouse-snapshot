Gavelhouse tracks Colorado under mandate states and says associations must disclose reserve balance and percent-funded status.

That second item is where boards often get thin.

A bank balance tells owners how much money exists today. Percent-funded status tells them how that money compares to the long-term reserve need.

Those are different questions.

Boards should keep both visible when they review reserves. A large balance can still be weak if the component obligations are larger.
