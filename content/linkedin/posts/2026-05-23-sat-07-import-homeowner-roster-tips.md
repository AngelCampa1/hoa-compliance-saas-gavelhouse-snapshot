Importing your homeowner list is the first task. Here is how to do it without creating a month of cleanup work.

The most common errors boards make when moving their roster to a new system:

**Duplicate entries.** Unit 12 has both the owner and a tenant listed with the same unit number. Now dues track to two records and neither is accurate.

**Stale contact info.** The list hasn't been updated since last spring. Three owners have sold. Two new owners have moved in. The new system starts with bad data and it takes months to notice.

**Missing unit-to-owner mapping.** The spreadsheet has names and emails but no unit numbers. Everything has to be matched manually.

**Inconsistent unit formats.** Some rows say "Unit 12," some say "#12," some say "12." The import tool treats them as three different units.

Before importing: deduplicate, standardize unit IDs, confirm ownership is current, and add a "moved in" date column so future audits have a reference point.

After importing: confirm the dues register is linked to the correct owners before sending the first automated reminder. A dues notice going to a former owner creates a bad first impression.

Most boards are up and running the same day. Getting the roster right before import is what makes that true.
