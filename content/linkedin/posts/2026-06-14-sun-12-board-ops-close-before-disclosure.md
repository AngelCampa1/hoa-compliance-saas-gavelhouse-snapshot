Reserve disclosures are weaker when the latest balances are not reviewed.

Before a board reports reserve information to owners, it should know the current operating balance, reserve balance, transfers, outstanding invoices, planned capital work, and whether the approved reserve contribution is posting as expected.

That is month-end close work.

Gavelhouse's product source includes board-ready reporting, audit packs, and month-end close as a trust signal. The reason is practical: clean disclosures start with clean internal records.

Owners can feel the difference.
