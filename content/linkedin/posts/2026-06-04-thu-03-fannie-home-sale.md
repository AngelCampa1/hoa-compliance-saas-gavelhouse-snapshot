Fannie Mae's LL-2026-03 is easy to treat like a lender document that lives outside board work.

That is a mistake.

The requirement says Full Review loan applications will require a 15% reserve allocation beginning January 4, 2027, and that underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals.

The board's reserve policy can become a homeowner's closing problem.

That does not mean every board needs to overreact. It means reserve funding should be discussed with the same seriousness as insurance, dues, and major maintenance.

The budget vote does not stay inside the boardroom. It affects owners who may need a buyer's loan to clear review.

Boards should know where they stand before that pressure arrives.
