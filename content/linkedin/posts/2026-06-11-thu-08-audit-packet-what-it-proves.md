An audit packet is more than a folder of statements.

For an HOA board, it should connect the financial records to board oversight: operating balance, reserve balance, approved budget, reserve allocation, close records, owner request audit trail where required, and the documents behind major decisions.

Gavelhouse includes audit-packet exports and a month-end close workflow as product features.

The reason is simple. If the board ever has to answer a financial question, it should not start by hunting through personal email accounts.
