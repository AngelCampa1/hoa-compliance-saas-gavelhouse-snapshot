January 4, 2027 is the effective date in Gavelhouse's compliance research. Waiting until January 2027 to react would be a board planning failure.

The budget that owners approve before then needs to account for the 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications.

That means the board should review the reserve allocation, compare it with the study or funding plan, and document the decision before the budget is adopted.

Lender deadlines become board deadlines when the budget has to change.
