The requirement says Hawaii requires 50% funded reserves under Act 296, with a 30-year horizon.

That kind of requirement cannot live in a static file.

The board needs to know the funding status, connect it to the reserve study, and keep the number visible as budgets change. Otherwise the rule is remembered only when someone prepares a disclosure or reviews old documents.

Percent funded is more than a reserve specialist's term.

It is a board operating metric, especially when state requirements and long-term funding plans are involved.
