Florida Chapter 718 requires condo and co-op buildings three stories or taller to complete a Structural Integrity Reserve Study every ten years.

Milestone inspections are required at 30 years, or 25 years for buildings within three miles of the coastline.

HB 913 (2025) addressed implementation questions from SB 4-D (2022) but did not remove or delay the core requirements. Boards that were watching for a rollback did not get one.

Florida also prohibits commingling of reserve and operating funds under FL Stat. §718.111(14) and §720.303. Penalties reach $5,000 per violation.

The reserve waiver that Florida boards previously used to avoid funding SIRS structural components is banned for those components. That waiver mechanism is gone for anything the SIRS covers.

For Florida condo boards, the question is not whether these requirements apply. It is whether the board has a current SIRS, a funding plan that actually funds the identified components, and separate accounts for reserve and operating funds.
