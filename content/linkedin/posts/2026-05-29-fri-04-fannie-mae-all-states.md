Texas has no state reserve mandate. Fannie Mae does not care.

Lender Letter LL-2026-03, effective January 4, 2027, applies to every association in every state. There is no exemption for states that do not require reserve studies. There is no carve-out for communities below a certain size.

If a buyer submits a Full Review loan application for a home in your community, the lender must verify that your association allocates at least 15% of its budget to reserves. Full stop.

This is a significant shift. Boards in states like Texas, Georgia, or Illinois, where state law imposes minimal or no reserve requirements, may believe they are operating fine. From a state compliance perspective, they may be right. From a mortgage eligibility perspective, they have a problem.

The Fannie Mae rule creates a federal floor that state law cannot waive. A board that has been waiving reserve funding for years, legally and within state guidelines, may now find its community financially unmarketable to buyers using conventional loans.

Seven months remain before January 4, 2027.
