A pattern shows up in self-managed communities: QuickBooks for transactions, a spreadsheet for reserve logic, and board minutes somewhere else.

That can work until it does not.

The spreadsheet explains which money is restricted, what the study recommends, and how future projects are planned. QuickBooks holds the accounting activity. The minutes hold the board decisions.

When those three records drift, the board loses the thread.

Reserve compliance should not depend on keeping side files mentally synced.
