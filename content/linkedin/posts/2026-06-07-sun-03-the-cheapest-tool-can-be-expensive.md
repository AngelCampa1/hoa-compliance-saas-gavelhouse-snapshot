Low-cost accounting software can look fine at first.

Then the board adds a spreadsheet for reserve components. A drive folder for meeting records. A calendar reminder for study deadlines. A payment export. A manually updated owner list. A second spreadsheet for delinquency tracking.

The price tag did not include the volunteer labor.

Gavelhouse's market research points to financial complexity as the number one reason volunteer boards hire professional management companies. That makes sense. At some point, the patched system becomes the work.

Gavelhouse's bet is that self-managed boards need purpose-built structure before they give up and outsource the whole operation.
