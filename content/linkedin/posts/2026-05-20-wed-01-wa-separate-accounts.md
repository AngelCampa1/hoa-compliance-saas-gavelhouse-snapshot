Washington state requires separate accounts by statute. RCW 64.34.364.

Not a best practice. Not a strong recommendation. A legal requirement.

Operating and reserve funds must be in structurally separate accounts. A single bank account with two rows in a spreadsheet does not satisfy RCW 64.34.364.

This is one of the cleaner statutory requirements in the country: the separation must be structural, not just labeled. The accounts must actually be separate.

Boards in Washington that are operating on a single combined account, or on QuickBooks with separate labels but not separate bank accounts, have a compliance issue they may not be aware of.

The statute doesn't ask why the accounts are combined. It doesn't care if the board inherited the structure from a prior treasurer or if the management software made it easy to keep things in one place. The requirement is the requirement.

Before your next board meeting, ask: are our operating and reserve funds in separate accounts? Not separate labels. Separate accounts at your financial institution, with separate account numbers.

If the answer is no, that's the first compliance item to fix.
