New Jersey Condominium Act (N.J.S.A. 46:8B-19) prohibits commingling of reserve and operating funds. The reserve balance can never fall below zero.

That is not a target. It is a hard prohibition.

S2760 (2024) and S3992 (2025) are adding baseline funding requirements on top of that floor. New Jersey is moving from a prohibition-only framework toward a mandate that will require associations to maintain a minimum funded level, not just avoid going negative.

The distinction matters for New Jersey boards. Under the current law, a community can be technically compliant by maintaining separate accounts and keeping the reserve balance above zero, even if that balance is $500 in a community that needs $2 million in reserves.

S2760 and S3992 are designed to close that gap. When they pass, the floor will be higher than zero.

New Jersey boards should understand their current funded percentage, not just their nominal reserve balance. A reserve balance that is positive but tiny is not a healthy reserve fund. It is a compliance technicality.
