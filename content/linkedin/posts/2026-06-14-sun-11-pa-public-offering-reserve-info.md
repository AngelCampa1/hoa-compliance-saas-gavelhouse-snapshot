Gavelhouse tracks Pennsylvania as a disclosure state and says reserve information must be included in the public offering statement.

That does not create the same operating pattern as a full reserve study mandate, but it still depends on accurate records.

Boards should know where the reserve information came from, when it was last reviewed, and whether the current budget still supports the story being told.

Disclosure is only as useful as the record behind it.

That is a board operations problem, more than a legal document problem.
