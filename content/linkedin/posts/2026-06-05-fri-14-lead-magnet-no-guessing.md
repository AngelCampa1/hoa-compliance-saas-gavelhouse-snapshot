Reserve conversations get messy when directors are working from different assumptions.

One person remembers a study cycle. Another remembers a disclosure rule. Someone else says the state does not require funding. Then Fannie Mae's 2027 reserve floor enters the conversation and nobody knows how it fits.

That is a bad way to make a budget decision.

The 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide gives boards a shared starting point: state reserve study and funding requirements, statutes, deadlines, penalties, and the Fannie Mae changes taking effect January 4, 2027.

It will not make the decision for the board.

It will make the first ten minutes of the conversation much less confused.
