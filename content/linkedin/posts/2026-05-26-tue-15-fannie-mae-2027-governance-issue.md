Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03, effective January 4, 2027, establishes a 15 percent reserve allocation requirement for Full Review loan applications.

The lending community will implement this. Lenders do not have discretion to waive Fannie Mae guidelines on conforming loans.

For HOA boards, this creates a governance obligation that sits alongside the fiduciary duty framework.

A board that knows about the January 4, 2027 deadline and chooses not to increase the reserve allocation to 15 percent before that date is making a documented governance decision. That decision will affect the ability of every homeowner in the community to sell to a buyer using a conventional mortgage.

The board minutes from the meeting where that decision is made, or not made, will be discoverable in any subsequent litigation.

Boards that get ahead of this have 19 months to adjust contribution rates, communicate the change to homeowners, and update the budget. Done over two budget cycles, the increase can be modest. Done the week before January 4, 2027, it cannot.

The question for every HOA board this budget season is whether the reserve allocation is already at 15 percent, and if not, what the path to 15 percent looks like.
