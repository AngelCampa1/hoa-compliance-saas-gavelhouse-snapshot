Percent funded can sound technical enough that boards leave it to the reserve specialist.

But several state entries in Gavelhouse's compliance research tie reserve disclosure to percent-funded status or reserve balance. California requires annual disclosure of percent-funded status. Colorado requires disclosure of reserve balance and percent-funded status.

The board does not need to become the specialist.

It does need to understand what the number means, whether it is changing, and how the approved budget affects it.

If the board cannot explain the trend, owners probably cannot either.
