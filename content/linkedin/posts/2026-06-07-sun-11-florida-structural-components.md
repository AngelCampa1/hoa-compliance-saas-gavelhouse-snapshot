Florida's section in Gavelhouse's compliance research is one of the clearest warnings for boards.

SIRS applies every 10 years for condos and co-ops three stories or taller. It also says Florida banned reserve waivers for structural components.

That changes the board conversation.

Deferral is no longer just a budget preference for covered structural work. The board needs to know which components are in the study, when the study is due, and how funding follows from it.

If that information is spread across old files and meeting minutes, the board is making the hardest decision with the weakest system.
