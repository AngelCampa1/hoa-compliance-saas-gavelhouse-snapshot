Because the financial record is where self-managed boards usually feel the most pressure.

Gavelhouse's market research points to financial complexity as the number one reason volunteer boards hire professional management companies, and says 58% of boards that made the switch cited finances as the trigger.

Community features matter. Owner communication matters. Governance workflows matter.

But if the board cannot separate reserve and operating funds, track study deadlines, close the month, and explain the budget, the rest sits on a weak base.

Compliance-first is a product priority decision.
