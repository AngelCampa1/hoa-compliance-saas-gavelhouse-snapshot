Gavelhouse tracks Connecticut as a disclosure state. It requires reserve fund balance disclosure in annual financial reports.

Even without a formal study mandate in that source entry, the board still needs clean records.

What is the reserve balance? Where is it held? How does it compare with expected capital needs? What did the board disclose last year? Can the next treasurer find the same number?

Disclosure rules are only simple when the underlying records are already organized.
