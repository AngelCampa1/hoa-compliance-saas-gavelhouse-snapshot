Open the board files and answer this without asking the treasurer:

Where is the latest reserve study? When is the next update due? Which account holds reserve cash? What was the last operating balance? What reserve allocation did the board approve? Where are the last close records?

If those answers are scattered, the board has an operating problem.

It may not be urgent today. It becomes urgent during turnover, budget season, owner records requests, lender reviews, or capital project approvals.

Clean files are quiet risk reduction.
