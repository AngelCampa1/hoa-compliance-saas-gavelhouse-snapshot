The requirement says New Jersey requires baseline funding that never drops below zero.

That sounds simple until a board has to operate it.

Every capital project, budget vote, reserve transfer, and year-end adjustment has to respect the floor. If the reserve account is tracked in a side spreadsheet, the board is depending on one person to catch the problem before it becomes official.

Boards should not rely on luck for that.

Reserve tracking belongs in the normal financial workflow, right beside operating cash, dues, reports, and board approvals.
