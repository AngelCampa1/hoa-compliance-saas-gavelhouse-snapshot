Gavelhouse tracks Hawaii under mandate states.

It cites HRS section 514B-148, a 50% funded requirement under Act 296 (2025), and a 30-year horizon under that same act.

That gives boards a concrete funding standard to manage against. It is not enough to say the association has a reserve account. The board needs to understand the funded position and whether the current plan supports it.

For a volunteer treasurer, this turns reserve funding into a recurring operating task.

What is the current balance? What does the study say? What is the funded percentage? Does the budget move the association toward compliance or away from it?

Those questions belong in the board workflow, not in a last-minute budget scramble.
