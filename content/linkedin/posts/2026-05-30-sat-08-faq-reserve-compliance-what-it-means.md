Boards ask: "Does Gavelhouse handle reserve fund compliance?" Yes. Here is what compliance-first actually means in practice.

Gavelhouse is compliance-first, with reserve tracking, fund separation, board-ready reporting, and workflow visibility built for self-managed boards.

That phrase is worth unpacking.

Reserve tracking means every reserve contribution is logged against the reserve fund specifically, not dumped into a single operating account. You can see the reserve balance at any point in time, and the history is clean enough to answer a lender questionnaire or a state audit request.

Fund separation means the operating fund and reserve fund are distinct at the database layer. Not two tabs in a spreadsheet. Not a memo field in QuickBooks. Structurally separate. California fines commingling up to $500 per day under CA Civ. Code §5510. Florida carries a $5,000 per violation penalty under FL Stat. §718.111(14). Separation is not optional in those states.

Board-ready reporting means the reports are formatted for a board meeting or a homeowner disclosure, not a CPA's working papers.

Workflow visibility means every board member can see reserve status without asking the treasurer to run a manual calculation.

Compliance-first means all of the above is the default, not a paid upgrade.
