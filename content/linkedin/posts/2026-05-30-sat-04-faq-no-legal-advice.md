Boards sometimes ask if Gavelhouse gives legal advice. We do not. Here is what we do instead.

Gavelhouse is a compliance operating tool. It tracks reserve contributions, separates operating and reserve funds, generates board-ready reports, and centralizes the financial workflows that self-managed volunteer boards run manually today.

What it does not do: interpret statutes, advise on whether a specific board action is legally permissible, or substitute for an attorney when your community faces litigation or regulatory action.

That line matters.

A board that asks "can we commingle reserves and operating funds?" needs a lawyer, not software. The answer from California law (CA Civ. Code §5510) is no, up to $500 per day in penalties. The answer from Florida law (FL Stat. §718.111(14)) is no, $5,000 per violation. But applying those statutes to a specific situation is legal work.

What Gavelhouse does is make it operationally easier to comply with the rules your attorney has already told you to follow. Separate accounts enforced at the database layer. Reserve allocation tracking you can run a report on. Documentation that holds up to a lender questionnaire or a state audit.

Operational compliance and legal advice are different services. We provide the first.
