State reserve rules are uneven.

California, Florida, Maryland, Washington, Hawaii, and other mandate states have specific requirements. Other states require disclosures. Some authorize reserves without a formal mandate. Many are silent.

Fannie Mae's January 4, 2027 change adds a separate layer for Full Review loan applications: 15% reserve allocation, regardless of state law.

This is exactly the kind of thing volunteer boards should not have to piece together from scattered tabs and old meeting packets.

The 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide puts the state-by-state reserve rules and the Fannie Mae deadline in one reference.

Use it before the next budget conversation, not after someone asks why a sale is stuck.
