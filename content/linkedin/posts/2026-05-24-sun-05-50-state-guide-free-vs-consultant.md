Before your board hires a compliance consultant to review reserve requirements, check whether the answer is already in a free document.

Most HOA boards face the same first question: what does our state actually require for reserve studies and funding? The answer is in the statutes. But finding it, reading it, and understanding what it means for your community takes time the board doesn't have.

A compliance consultant can answer this. So can the "50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide."

What the guide covers:

- Every state's reserve study requirements, with statute citations
- Funding mandates and deadline schedules
- Annual disclosure requirements where they apply
- Penalties for non-compliance where documented
- The Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 changes effective January 4, 2027

What it doesn't replace: an actual reserve study from a qualified reserve analyst, legal advice for a specific dispute, or an accountant review of your financials.

But as a starting point, as the document you use to ask better questions and know whether your community is in the right ballpark, it covers the ground a consultant charges for in the first two hours.
