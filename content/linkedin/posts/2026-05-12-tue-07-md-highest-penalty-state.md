Maryland HB 292 (2025) made reserve funding mandatory. Disclosure alone is no longer the standard.

HB 107 (2022, Chapter 664) required Maryland associations to commission reserve studies. HB 292 (2025, Chapter 519, effective October 1, 2025) shifts Maryland from disclosure to mandatory funding. Associations must adopt a funding plan in consultation with the reserve study author and deposit the recommended reserve contributions into the reserve account by the end of each fiscal year.

The reserve provisions for HOAs sit in Md. Real Prop. §11B-112.2 (annual budget reserve requirements) and §11B-112.3 (reserve study requirements). Condominium reserve studies are governed by §11-109.4.

Enforcement runs through the Maryland Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division under the Maryland Condominium Act and Homeowners Association Act. The combination of a mandatory funding plan and AG enforcement authority means Maryland HOA boards now have a concrete, auditable obligation, not a disclosure-only obligation.

If your Maryland board has not reviewed its reserve study, funding plan, and budget alignment since HB 292 took effect, that review is the right place to start.
