The single most common source of board email: homeowners asking about their balance, their request status, or when the board last made a decision about something.

Every one of those emails requires a board member to look something up and reply. Multiply that by 80 homeowners and it becomes a part-time job.

An owner portal solves this by giving homeowners a self-service layer. They can check their dues balance, see the status of an open request, and review information the board has shared, all without emailing anyone.

This matters beyond convenience. Florida's §720.303(5) requires boards to keep owner request records accessible. California's §4525 has owner access provisions that overlap with this. The portal isn't just an operational convenience: in some states it's part of the compliance picture.

Boards that give owners visibility get fewer angry emails. Not because the owners are less engaged, but because they can find the answer themselves instead of waiting three days for a reply.

Gavelhouse's owner portal is included in every plan. Homeowners get their own login, separate from the board login, with access only to their own information and board-shared records.
