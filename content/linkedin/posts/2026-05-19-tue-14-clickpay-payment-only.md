ClickPay processes payments. It does not manage your HOA. These are different things.

ClickPay is a payment processing platform. Contact for pricing. No HOA management features. No reserve fund tracking. No compliance reporting. No accounting.

Payment processing is a component of HOA management, not a substitute for it. Collecting dues matters. But a board that uses ClickPay for dues and a spreadsheet for everything else has payment infrastructure without management infrastructure.

The questions that determine board liability are not answered by payment processing:
- Are operating and reserve funds structurally separated?
- Does the reserve fund meet the state's minimum funding requirement?
- Has the board completed a reserve study within the legally required cycle?
- Can the board produce a percent-funded disclosure for homeowners?

A payment processor can tell you whether dues were collected. It cannot tell you whether the reserve fund is compliant.

When evaluating HOA software, the categories matter. Payment processing, website hosting, accounting, reserve compliance, and full HOA management are not the same thing and are not interchangeable.

Know what you're buying.
