Gavelhouse tracks Oregon's reserve study frequency as every 3 to 5 years and says the study must include a funding plan.

The funding plan is where board discipline shows up.

It is one thing to know the community has future component costs. It is another to approve assessments that line up with those costs.

When boards review the budget, they should compare it with the funding plan directly.

Otherwise, the study becomes a document the association paid for but does not operate from.
