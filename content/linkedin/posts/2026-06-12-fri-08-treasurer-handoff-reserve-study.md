If a new treasurer receives bank access but not the reserve study, the handoff is incomplete.

The study explains why reserve contributions exist, which components the association is planning for, when the next update is due, and whether the current funding plan still makes sense.

Without it, the treasurer is left managing cash without context.

That is how boards drift into bad habits: reserve transfers become arbitrary, capital projects feel like surprises, and dues conversations lose their factual base.

Handoff the study with the accounts.
