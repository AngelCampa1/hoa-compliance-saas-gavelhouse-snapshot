January 4, 2027 is 237 days away.

Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 sets a 15% reserve allocation floor for Full Review loan applications. Starting that date, associations below the floor can affect mortgage approval for homeowners trying to sell.

This is not a suggestion. It applies to every association in every state, regardless of what your state's reserve statute says.

Here is what that means practically. A homeowner in your community finds a buyer. The buyer's lender orders a Full Review condo questionnaire. If your association's reserve allocation comes back below 15%, the loan may not get approved. The sale falls through. The homeowner blames the board.

That outcome is now less than 240 days away for underfunded communities, and many boards do not know their current reserve allocation percentage.
