The compliance research includes a sharp line: "Financial complexity is the #1 reason volunteer boards hire professional management companies."

That is believable because finance is where the board's informal systems stop working.

Dues have to be tracked. Delinquencies need follow-up. Reserve and operating funds have to stay separate. Reports need to make sense. The next treasurer needs to understand what happened before.

When those jobs live across disconnected tools, the board is managing the system instead of the community.

Gavelhouse gives self-managed boards a way to keep the structure without handing over the whole operation.
