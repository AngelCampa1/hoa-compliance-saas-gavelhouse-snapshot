Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 establishes a 15 percent reserve allocation requirement for HOA associations under Full Review loan applications.

Effective date: January 4, 2027.

That is less than eight months away.

The requirement applies to every association in every state, regardless of whether the state has a reserve mandate. Silent states, permissive states, and disclosure states all fall under the Fannie Mae policy.

For communities where the reserve allocation is currently below 15 percent, the budget adjustment path runs through one or two annual budget cycles. If the 2026 budget is already set, the 2027 budget cycle is the next opportunity.

A community with 10 percent reserve allocation today that waits until December 2026 to address the shortfall cannot close a five-percentage-point budget gap in a few weeks. Budget changes require homeowner notice, board votes, and assessment adjustment periods that vary by state and governing documents.

The communities that will be compliant on January 4, 2027 are the ones making budget decisions now, in the spring and summer of 2026, for the next fiscal year.

The communities that will not be compliant are the ones that will still be discussing whether the Fannie Mae policy applies to them after the effective date has passed.
