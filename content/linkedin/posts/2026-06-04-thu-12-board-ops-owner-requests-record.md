An owner request can start small.

A document question. A payment question. A records request. A concern about a repair or reserve item.

If the board handles it through scattered inboxes, the record gets messy fast. Who received it? Who answered? What was provided? Was the response tied to a governing document, meeting action, or financial record?

Gavelhouse includes an owner portal with request visibility and audit trail.

That is not about making the board more bureaucratic. It is about protecting volunteers from having to reconstruct basic facts after the tone changes.

Good records lower the temperature because everyone can see the path of the request.

Self-managed boards need that structure as much as larger organizations do.
