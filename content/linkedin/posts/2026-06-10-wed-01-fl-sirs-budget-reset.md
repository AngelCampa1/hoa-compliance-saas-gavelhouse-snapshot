Florida's Structural Integrity Reserve Study rule is easy to treat like a paperwork item. It is not.

For condos and co-ops three stories or taller, the study has to happen every 10 years. The hard part comes after the report lands: the budget has to reflect the structural components the study identifies.

That changes the treasurer's job. Reserve funding is no longer a line the board can casually defer when the annual budget feels tight.

The study creates the obligation. The budget has to show how the association will meet it.
