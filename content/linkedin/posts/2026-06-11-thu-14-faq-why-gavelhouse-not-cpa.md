No.

Gavelhouse is operating software for self-managed boards. It is not a substitute for licensed professional advice.

Gavelhouse positions Gavelhouse around financial control, governance workflows, owner operations, and compliance tracking. That means the product helps the board keep records organized, separate funds, track deadlines, close periods, and produce board-ready reports.

A reserve specialist still prepares the reserve study. A CPA still gives accounting advice. An attorney still interprets legal questions.

Software should make the board's day-to-day process cleaner. It should not pretend to be a professional license.
