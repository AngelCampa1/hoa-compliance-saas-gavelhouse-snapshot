Fannie Mae's 15% reserve allocation requirement applies to Full Review loan applications starting January 4, 2027.

That is the kind of phrase a board can easily ignore because it sounds like lender language.

But the impact can land on homeowners. The practical risk is that underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals.

So the board question is practical: if a lender reviews the association next year, what will the budget, reserve allocation, and records show?

That answer should not depend on one volunteer remembering where the files are.
