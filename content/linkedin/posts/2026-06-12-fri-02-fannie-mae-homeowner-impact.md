Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 is easy to file under "lender rules."

That is too narrow.

The requirement says underfunded associations could block homeowner mortgage approvals when the 15% reserve allocation floor applies to Full Review loan applications starting January 4, 2027.

That means a board's reserve budget can affect an owner's ability to buy, sell, or refinance.

The board may never touch the mortgage application. Its records can still become part of the answer.
