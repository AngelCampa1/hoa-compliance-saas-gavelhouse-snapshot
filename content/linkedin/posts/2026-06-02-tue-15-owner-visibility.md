Gavelhouse tracks owner portal visibility and governance records with audit trail in the Growth plan.

This matters because owner requests are not merely customer service. They are records. The board needs to know what was requested, when it came in, who responded, and what document or decision was used.

For a self-managed board, this is where informal operations get expensive.

One director has the email. Another has the meeting notes. The treasurer has the payment record. The homeowner sees silence and assumes the board is hiding something.

Better visibility does not mean every owner gets every internal board note. It means the board has a structured way to handle requests without losing the trail.

That is part of running a community, not a bonus feature.
