If a volunteer board cannot explain the pricing in a meeting, the sale gets harder.

Gavelhouse's self-serve annual plans are built around community size: Starter up to 50 homes, Growth for 51 to 200, and Scale for 201 to 500. Portfolio is custom for larger or multi-community cases.

No per-unit fees on any plan.

That structure matters because board approval is public, practical, and usually time-boxed. People want to know what the association pays and what changes as the community grows.

Clear pricing is not a marketing flourish. It is part of the governance process.
