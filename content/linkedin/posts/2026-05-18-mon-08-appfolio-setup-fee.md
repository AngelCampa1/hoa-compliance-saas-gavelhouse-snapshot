AppFolio charges a $400+ setup fee before you've done anything.

Then $280/month minimum. Then $0.80 to $5 per unit on top of that.

For a 100-unit community, you're looking at $280 to $780+ per month depending on the unit rate, plus the setup cost just to get started.

AppFolio was built for mid-to-large professional management companies running hundreds of units across multiple properties. That's their market. The pricing reflects it.

A self-managed volunteer HOA board is not their customer. The feature set assumes staff accountants and property managers. The pricing assumes professional management revenue justifies the cost.

Gavelhouse charges $0 to set up and about $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 200 homes. It is built for self-managed boards. No per-unit fees. No setup cost. 30-day money-back guarantee.

These are different tools for different customers. The problem is when boards end up paying enterprise management company prices for a product category they don't need.
