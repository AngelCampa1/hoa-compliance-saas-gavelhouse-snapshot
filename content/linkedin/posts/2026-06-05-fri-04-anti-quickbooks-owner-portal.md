QuickBooks can help a treasurer record money movement.

It does not give the board an owner request workflow with visibility and audit trail. Gavelhouse includes that in Growth, tied to owner operations and governance records.

For self-managed boards, the owner side matters because financial questions often start as requests.

Can I see the budget? Why did dues change? What is the reserve balance? When was the study updated? Where is the document for that decision?

If those questions are handled only through email and accounting exports, the board has to stitch the story together manually.

The financial record and owner request record should be close enough that the board can answer with confidence.
