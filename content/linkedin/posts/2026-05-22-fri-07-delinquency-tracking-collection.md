One unpaid homeowner is a minor issue. Six unpaid homeowners with no tracking system is a cash flow problem.

The boards that end up in collection chaos are almost never the ones with a malicious homeowner. They're the ones who let the process go unmanaged.

Here's how it happens:

Month one: Dues go unpaid. The treasurer notices but doesn't send a formal notice.

Month two: Still unpaid. The treasurer sends an email. No record in the system.

Month three: The board asks what's happening. Nobody can find the email thread. The homeowner claims they never received anything.

Month six: The board is considering a lien and has no clean paper trail to support it.

Delinquency tracking isn't about being aggressive with homeowners. It's about having a record that shows exactly when notices were sent, what amounts are outstanding, and what the board did about it. That record protects the board if the situation escalates.

Gavelhouse tracks dues, automates reminders, and keeps the delinquency history in one place so the board can always show what steps were taken.
