CA Civ. Code §5510 prohibits commingling reserve and operating funds. The fine is $100-$500 per day.

That is not per incident. That is per day the violation continues. A 90-day audit cycle with commingled accounts can produce a penalty in the tens of thousands before anyone files a formal complaint.

The Davis-Stirling Act (Civil Code §5550-5560) also requires California boards to commission a reserve study every three years, with a visual inspection annually. The results must be disclosed to every member along with a 30-year funding plan.

SB 900 (2024), AB 2114 (2024), and SB 410 (2025) have continued to tighten California's requirements. If your board last reviewed your reserve compliance posture before 2024, a fresh review is overdue.

California is a mandate state. Compliance is not optional, and the disclosure requirements are specific enough that "we did not know" is not a defense.
