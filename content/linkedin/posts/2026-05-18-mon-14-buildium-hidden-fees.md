Buildium's base price is $62 to $400 per month depending on tier.

The real cost is 30 to 50 percent higher after add-ons.

Buildium was built for professional property management companies. Their pricing model reflects that: a base subscription plus fees for additional features, transaction processing, and premium support. Those add-on costs accumulate quickly.

For a professional management company with revenue from management fees, the total cost can be justified. For a volunteer HOA board spending from a community operating budget, the add-on structure creates unpredictability.

Volunteer boards often have fixed budgets approved annually. A software subscription that could be $150 one month and $240 the next due to variable add-ons creates a budget problem.

Buildium is also built for professional management companies, not self-managed boards. The feature set, the pricing structure, and the onboarding process assume staff who do this full-time.

If your board manages your own community, you're not Buildium's target customer. That's not a criticism of Buildium. It's a product category distinction that matters when you're choosing tools.
