How much does Gavelhouse cost? Here is the full breakdown, with no fine print.

Annual billing is the default. Starter is $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for communities up to 50 homes. Growth is $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for 51 to 200 homes. Month-to-month is available if your board prefers it. No annual contract required.

No per-unit fees. No setup fee. 30-day money-back guarantee.
