Gavelhouse tracks Delaware's reserve study requirement at least every 5 years and says the study must include a funding plan.

That second part is the board work.

A component list without a funding plan tells you what can break. It does not tell you how the association intends to pay for the work.

Boards should treat the funding plan as a living reference during budget review. If assessments do not match the plan, the board should know why and document the decision.
