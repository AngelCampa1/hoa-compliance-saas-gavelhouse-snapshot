Owner requests are where board operations get messy fast.

Someone asks for records. Someone asks about dues. Someone wants visibility into a request. The board answers in a text thread, an email chain, or a side conversation after a meeting.

That might work until there is a dispute.

Gavelhouse describes Gavelhouse's owner portal and governance records with audit trail on owner requests. That is the right shape for this work: the request, the response, the timing, and the record should stay together.

Boards should not need a memory contest to prove what happened.
