January 4, 2027 can sound far away until the board remembers how budgets work.

Reserve funding changes rarely happen cleanly at the last minute. Dues notices, owner questions, reserve study updates, and meeting approvals all take time.

The requirement says Fannie Mae's LL-2026-03 will require a 15% reserve allocation for Full Review loan applications beginning on that date.

A board that waits until the rule is effective has fewer choices.

The practical move is to look at the reserve allocation during the next budget cycle. What is the current allocation? What does the reserve study say? What would a 15% floor mean for the association?

Those are board questions now, not merely lender questions later.
