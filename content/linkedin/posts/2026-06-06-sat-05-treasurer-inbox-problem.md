The treasurer's inbox usually knows more than the board packet.

Vendor questions, bank messages, owner payment issues, reserve study notes, delinquency reminders, budget drafts. It all lands in one person's email, then everyone hopes the handoff goes smoothly when that person leaves.

That is not a character flaw. It is a systems flaw.

A board operating system should make the important record live outside one volunteer's inbox. The next treasurer should see the account structure, open items, deadlines, and prior close without reconstructing history from forwarded messages.

That is the standard self-managed boards should expect.
