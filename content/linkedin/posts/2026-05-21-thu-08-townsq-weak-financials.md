TownSq is $90/month for up to 300 units. Reserve tracking is only available on the Enterprise tier.

A 200-unit community paying $90/month for TownSq may not have reserve tracking included in their plan.

This is the compliance tier problem: the software exists, the feature exists, but access to the feature requires an upgrade. For the communities that need reserve compliance most, the tool they're paying for may not cover it.

At 300 units, missing reserve compliance is a serious gap. A community that size likely has six figures in reserve fund obligations. The statutory requirements for reserve studies, percent-funded disclosures, and fund separation don't scale down because the software tier doesn't include them.

The alternative framing: if a software platform considers reserve tracking an enterprise feature, what does that say about how they prioritize compliance for smaller communities?

Gavelhouse treats reserve compliance as a core feature on every plan. A 30-unit community on Starter at about $10/month billed annually with Y80OFF and a 400-unit community on Scale at about $50/month billed annually with Y80OFF both get the same reserve compliance coverage.

Reserve compliance is not an upgrade. It's the reason the software exists.
