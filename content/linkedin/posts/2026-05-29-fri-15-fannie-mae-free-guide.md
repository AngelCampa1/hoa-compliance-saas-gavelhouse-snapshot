We put together a free resource that maps every state's reserve requirements alongside the Fannie Mae 2027 rule.

It is called the 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide.

What is in it: every state's reserve study and funding requirements, the relevant statutes, deadlines, penalty structures, and the full details on Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and what it requires before January 4, 2027.

We built Gavelhouse because we kept seeing self-managed volunteer boards operating without any of this information organized in one place. They were relying on whatever their state's HOA association mailed them, or whatever they found on Google, or whatever their attorney told them three years ago.

The Fannie Mae rule changes the stakes. Communities below 15% reserve allocation will face mortgage eligibility problems for their homeowners starting January 4, 2027. That is a federal floor that does not care what your state law says.

The guide is free. No paywall, no credit card.

Download it at gavelhouse.app/free/ and share it with your board before your next meeting.
