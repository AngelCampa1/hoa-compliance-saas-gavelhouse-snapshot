Owner visibility is more than a courtesy. In some states, owner records and requests sit inside specific compliance obligations.

Gavelhouse includes owner portal request visibility and audit trail in Growth, with Florida and California owner record contexts in mind.

For a volunteer board, this matters most when financial questions appear.

Who asked for the records? When did the board respond? What was provided? Which reserve or budget documents were involved?

A clean request trail protects both sides from confusion.
