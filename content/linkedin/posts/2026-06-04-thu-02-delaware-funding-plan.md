Gavelhouse tracks Delaware under mandate states.

It cites Delaware Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act section 81-318, says the reserve study is required at least every five years, and says the study must include a funding plan.

That is the part boards should not skip over.

A study without a funding plan is a list of future problems. A funding plan turns that list into an operating commitment the board has to discuss, budget for, and explain.

For self-managed communities, the risk is rarely that nobody cares. The risk is that the funding plan gets separated from normal board work.

The plan belongs next to the budget, dues assumptions, reserve balance, and meeting record.

Otherwise it becomes another document the board respects in theory and forgets in practice.
