The 50-State HOA Reserve Fund Requirements Guide covers what each state mandates, recommends, or is silent on, across three core questions:

1. Is a reserve study required, and how often?
2. Is commingling of reserve and operating funds prohibited, and what is the penalty?
3. What is the state's funding standard (mandatory, baseline, or none)?

This guide was built for the treasurer who inherits a community with no documentation, for the board member trying to understand whether their state's rules actually apply to them, and for the incoming secretary who just discovered the reserve account has not been touched in four years.

It does not replace a licensed reserve specialist or your state's governing documents. It is a starting point: what the statute says, cited by section, so you can verify it yourself or take it to your attorney.
