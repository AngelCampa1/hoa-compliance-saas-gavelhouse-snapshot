The Fannie Mae change is plain:

Lender Letter LL-2026-03 takes effect January 4, 2027. Full Review loan applications will require a 15% reserve allocation. The rule applies to all associations regardless of state law.

That means a board can be in a state with light reserve statutes and still run into a reserve problem when an owner tries to sell or refinance.

This is why reserve funding cannot live as a once-a-year budget footnote. It touches the marketability of homes inside the association.

Board members do not need to become lending specialists. They do need to know whether their current budget and reserve plan leave homeowners exposed when the 2027 rule arrives.
