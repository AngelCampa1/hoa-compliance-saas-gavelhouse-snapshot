Month-end close sounds like something only accountants care about.

For a self-managed HOA, it is one of the simplest board controls available.

The board should know the operating balance, the reserve balance, open invoices, delinquent assessments, and whether the reserve allocation matches the approved budget. Someone should attest that those numbers were reviewed.

Gavelhouse's Scale plan includes a month-end close workflow with attested balances, under Gavelhouse's compliance research.

This matters because a dated review record is harder to fake after a problem appears.
