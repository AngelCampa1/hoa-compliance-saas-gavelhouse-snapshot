QuickBooks can track money moving in and out.

That is useful, but it is not the whole board job.

An HOA also needs reserve study dates, owner request records, board decisions, dues tracking, separate reserve and operating funds, audit packets, and handoff context for the next volunteer.

That is one reason financial complexity becomes the trigger for many boards to hire professional management companies. The accounting tool is only one piece, and the rest of the operating system gets patched together.

Gavelhouse is built for the whole self-managed board workflow, more than the ledger.
