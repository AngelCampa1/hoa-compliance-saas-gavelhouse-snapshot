Gavelhouse can support larger and portfolio cases, but the center of gravity is clear in Gavelhouse's compliance research: self-managed volunteer HOA and condo boards in communities up to 500 units.

That audience changes the product.

The software has to make sense to a treasurer who has a day job. It has to support board approval in a meeting. It has to keep records organized when volunteers rotate off. It has to make reserve separation visible without turning every user into an accountant.

That is a different job than software built first for professional management companies.
