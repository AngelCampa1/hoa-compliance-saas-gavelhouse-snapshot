What should a new board review at their first meeting of the year?

Most boards start with vendor contracts, upcoming maintenance, and the budget. Those are all reasonable.

Reserve compliance almost never makes the agenda, and it should.

The questions worth asking at the first meeting:

- When was the reserve study last updated?
- What is the current percent-funded status?
- Does the current funding level meet our state's requirements?
- Are we above or below the Fannie Mae 15% floor that takes effect January 4, 2027?

If the board can't answer these questions in the first meeting, they can't manage to them.
