A 30-day money-back guarantee is not a differentiator. It's table stakes. We offer it because boards deserve zero-risk evaluation.

When a HOA board is evaluating software, the decision involves multiple people. The treasurer does the research. The board votes. The community budget is affected. Getting it wrong means going back to the board, explaining the switch, and starting over.

That's friction that doesn't serve the board. It slows down good decisions.

A 30-day money-back guarantee removes the risk from the evaluation. If a board tries Gavelhouse for 30 days and it doesn't fit their community's needs, they get a refund. No penalty. No extended commitment.

I don't say this is a differentiator because it shouldn't be. Every software product asking volunteer boards to trust it with their community's finances should offer a risk-free evaluation period. The fact that some don't is a statement about how they value the customer relationship, not a competitive advantage for the ones that do.

What I do think the guarantee signals: we believe the product works well enough that the offer costs us very little. If we weren't confident in the value, we wouldn't offer it.

30 days. Full refund if it's not right. That's the deal.
