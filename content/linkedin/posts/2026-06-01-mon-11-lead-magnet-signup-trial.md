If your board is evaluating HOA financial software, here is what the Gavelhouse pricing model looks like.

**Flat pricing.** Starter, Growth, and Scale are priced by community size, not per unit. With Y80OFF, annual effective prices are about $10, $27, and $50 per month when billed annually. Portfolio is custom. Monthly billing uses M80OFF for 80% off the first year.

**No per-unit fees.** A volunteer board should be able to understand the software cost before it votes, without running a spreadsheet for every home count.

**What the board gets.** Reserve and operating fund separation enforced at the database layer, rather than just a UI label. State-specific reserve study deadline tracking. Month-end close workflow with attested balances. Audit-packet exports.

**What the board can evaluate.** Whether the system gives the treasurer a cleaner close, gives directors a better audit trail, and gives owners clearer records before the board approves a purchase.

**What Gavelhouse is built for.** Self-managed volunteer boards, communities up to 500 units. Not professional management companies.
