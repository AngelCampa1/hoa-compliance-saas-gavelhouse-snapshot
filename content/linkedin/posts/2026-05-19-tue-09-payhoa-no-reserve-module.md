PayHOA starts at $49/month for up to 25 units.

It has no dedicated reserve study module. Reserve tracking is handled partially through accounting features, not through a purpose-built compliance workflow.

For small communities where the reserve fund is simple and the state requirements are minimal, that may be sufficient.

For communities in California, Florida, Maryland, or New Jersey with statutory disclosure requirements, a mandated reserve study cycle, and commingling penalties, "partial reserve tracking through accounting" leaves a compliance gap.

A reserve study module is not a feature upgrade. For communities in states with mandatory reserve study requirements, it's a statutory obligation. The tool you use should be able to produce what your state requires, not roughly mimic it through a general accounting ledger.

Before choosing any HOA platform, ask specifically: can this tool generate a reserve study that meets my state's requirements? Can it track percent funded against my state's disclosure threshold? Does it enforce the structural separation of operating and reserve funds?

The answer determines whether you have HOA management software or billing software with an HOA label.
