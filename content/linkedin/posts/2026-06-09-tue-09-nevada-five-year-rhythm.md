Nevada's section in Gavelhouse's compliance research is direct: NRS §116.31152, reserve study every 5 years.

A five-year rhythm is long enough for boards to forget and short enough for the deadline to matter.

That is exactly the kind of requirement a self-managed association should not track informally. Board members rotate. Treasurers change. Old calendars get lost.

The system should remember the next study date and keep the current reserve work visible between updates.

Compliance gets easier when the board does not have to rediscover the cycle every term.
