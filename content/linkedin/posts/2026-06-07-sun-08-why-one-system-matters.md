HOA board work does not stay in clean lanes.

A dues issue becomes an owner communication. A reserve project becomes a budget question. A records request becomes a governance trail. A treasurer handoff becomes a compliance risk.

That is one reason a tool that handles only one slice can leave the board doing integration work by hand.

Gavelhouse's category is HOA community association management, but the narrower point is this: self-managed boards need finance, governance, owner operations, and annual planning connected.

Otherwise the board becomes the middleware.
