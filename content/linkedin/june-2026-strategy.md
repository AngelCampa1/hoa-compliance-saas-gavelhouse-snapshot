# June 2026 LinkedIn Strategy

The organic LinkedIn push did not work. Here are the numbers and what we
planned next.

Source: the LinkedIn company-page analytics export.

## Data Read

The analytics window is small. The Metrics sheet covers 04/18/2026 to
05/17/2026, but only 05/12 to 05/17 had nonzero activity. The All posts sheet
has 80 organic posts created during that same active window.

Metrics totals: 2,559 impressions, 1,886 unique organic impressions, 4 clicks,
4 reactions, 0 comments, 0 reposts, and 0.31% weighted engagement.

Post-level totals: 3,399 impressions, 7 clicks, 4 likes, 0 comments, 0 reposts,
11 posts with any engagement, and 69 posts with zero engagement. The signal is
directional, not conclusive.

## Trend

State-law urgency and the Fannie Mae LL-2026-03 angle drove reach. The sparse
engagement came from more practical pain: QuickBooks and fund separation,
board liability, budget season, and Maryland penalty risk. Broad countdown
posts reached people but did not create much interaction on their own.

## June 1-14 Mix

The June 1-14 prepared batch keeps the cadence at 15 posts per day, 210 posts
total. The mix is intentionally heavier on state compliance and Fannie Mae
because those topics earned the most reach, but it keeps repeating operational
angles because the engagement signal was stronger there.

Pillar mix in the prepared manifest:

| Pillar | Posts |
|---|---:|
| state-compliance | 56 |
| board-ops | 31 |
| fannie-mae | 25 |
| reserve-mechanics | 19 |
| board-liability | 18 |
| builder-pov | 17 |
| faq | 16 |
| lead-magnet | 15 |
| anti-quickbooks | 13 |

Daily structure: lead with state, reserve, or Fannie Mae urgency in morning
slots; rotate practical board operations and liability through midday; use FAQ,
builder perspective, and guide CTAs later in the day. The goal is not to make
new claims from thin data. It is to keep testing the strongest early themes
while preserving a practical, board-facing voice.
