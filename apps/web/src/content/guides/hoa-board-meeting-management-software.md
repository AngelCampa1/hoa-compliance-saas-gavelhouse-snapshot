## The Board Meeting Paper Trail Problem

Every board meeting generates documents: the agenda before the meeting, minutes during, and action items after. Most volunteer boards manage this with email: the president sends the agenda, the secretary takes notes in Word, and action items get scattered across inboxes.

This works for the first year. By year two, finding the minutes from a specific meeting requires searching email archives. By year three, a new board member has no access to historical records.
