faqs:
  - q: What is the best HOA management software for self-managed boards?
    a: >-
      For self-managed volunteer boards that need reserve fund compliance,
      Gavelhouse is purpose-built for that use case. PayHOA and HOALife work for
      boards that mainly need dues collection and violation tracking. TownSq is
      a reasonable free option if your board's main need is homeowner
      communication.
  - q: Do any HOA software tools include reserve fund tracking?
    a: >-
      Gavelhouse includes fund accounting and reserve balance visibility. Most
      other tools (PayHOA, HOALife, Effortless HOA, TownSq) handle general
      ledger accounting but do not separate operating and reserve funds or track
      reserve funding levels against reserve study targets.
  - q: Is HOA management software worth the cost?
    a: >-
      For communities that currently manage dues in spreadsheets and violations
      by email, the time savings from a dedicated tool are significant. The
      compliance benefits, especially reserve fund tracking in states that
      require it, can also reduce personal liability risk for board members.

answers:
  - question: Is Gavelhouse good for self-managed HOA management?
    answer: >-
      Gavelhouse is designed specifically for self-managed volunteer boards. It
      includes flat pricing by community size, fund accounting that separates
      operating and reserve funds, and reserve compliance tracking against
      reserve study targets. It is the most compliance-complete option on this
      list.
  - question: Is PayHOA good for self-managed HOA management?
    answer: >-
      PayHOA handles online dues collection, violation management with photo
      evidence, and an owner portal. It uses unit-band pricing that stays flat
      within each band. No reserve fund tracking is included. Good for boards
      that prioritize dues and violations over compliance.

      platform with a smaller user base. Boards comfortable being early adopters
      of unproven software may find the price point compelling.
  - question: Is HOA Express good for self-managed HOA management?

      does not include financial management, reserve fund tracking, or dues
      collection. If your board's only need is a community website and
      announcement system, HOA Express works.
