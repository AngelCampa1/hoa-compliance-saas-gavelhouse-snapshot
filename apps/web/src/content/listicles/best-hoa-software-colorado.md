## Why Colorado HOA Software Selection Changed in 2024

Colorado's HOA regulatory environment shifted with HB 24-1233. The bill expanded financial transparency requirements on top of the existing Common Interest Ownership Act framework. The practical effect for boards: more required disclosures, more granular reserve fund reporting, and tighter expectations around what owners can request from the association.

Pre-2024, a Colorado HOA could comply with CCIOA disclosure requirements using QuickBooks-generated financial statements and a separate reserve study. Post-HB 24-1233, the disclosure shape is more specific, and the gap between "raw financial reports" and "HB 24-1233 disclosure" is wider.

Software either fills that gap automatically or leaves the volunteer board to do it by hand each year. For 50-200 unit communities in Denver, Aurora, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, and the mountain resort communities, the volunteer-hour cost of manual disclosure preparation is the practical cost of choosing software that does not handle HB 24-1233 natively.

## What Colorado Boards Should Look For

**HB 24-1233 disclosure templates.** Reserve study summary, current reserve balance, funding plan, recent assessments — all in the form required by the statute.

**Reserve study compliance tracking.** CCIOA 38-33.3-209.5 requires reserve study maintenance and annual review. Software that ingests study targets and tracks current funding against them makes the annual review routine.

**Fund accounting at the ledger level.** Operating and reserve funds must be separately accounted for. CCIOA and HB 24-1233 both reinforce this; software that allows commingling creates audit risk.

**Document retention for annual disclosure cycle.** Meeting minutes, notices, financial reports, reserve studies — all need to be retained and producible on owner request. HB 24-1233 expanded the request rights.

### 2. PayHOA — Best for Dues and Violations

PayHOA is a strong day-to-day tool. Online dues collection, homeowner payment portals, and violation management with photo evidence cover the bulk of weekly Colorado HOA workload. For Aurora and Colorado Springs planned communities focused on dues collection and CC&R enforcement, PayHOA handles the operational surface area.

The reserve compliance and HB 24-1233 disclosure gaps are real. Operating and reserve balances live in a single ledger, and there is no built-in disclosure-report generation. Boards using PayHOA in Colorado typically produce HB 24-1233 disclosures manually or via a separate accounting tool.

### 3. Condo Control — Best for Denver and Boulder Condos

For Denver and Boulder mid-size condo associations with shared amenities — gyms, pools, rooftop decks — Condo Control's full-suite operational features fit. Amenity booking, visitor management, and document storage cover the operational shape of a 100-unit downtown condo.

Like other full-suite platforms, Condo Control does not include dedicated CCIOA reserve compliance tracking or HB 24-1233 disclosure templates. The financial module produces reports the board can then format into the required disclosures.
