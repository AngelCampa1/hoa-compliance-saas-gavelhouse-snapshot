## Communication Is Necessary But Not Sufficient

Every HOA board needs a way to communicate with homeowners: announcements, document distribution, and messaging. Most boards start with email lists or a Facebook group. These work for basic announcements but create problems with document management, record retention, and homeowner privacy.

Dedicated HOA communication platforms solve the messaging problem. The question is whether messaging alone justifies another software subscription, or whether boards should choose a platform that integrates communication with financial management and compliance.

Gavelhouse is the top recommendation when communication cannot be separated from the board's financial record. It includes homeowner communication alongside fund accounting and reserve tracking, starting at $10/month billed annually with Y80OFF.
