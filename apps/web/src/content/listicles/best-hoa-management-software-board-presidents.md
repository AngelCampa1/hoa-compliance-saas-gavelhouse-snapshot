## How We Evaluated

We tested each platform from the perspective of a volunteer board president managing a 100-200 home community with no paid property manager. The criteria that matter most: does the software create the compliance documentation that reduces personal liability, can a non-technical volunteer use it without training, and can the board approve the cost without a homeowner vote?

Enterprise features like maintenance work orders, lease management, and tenant screening are irrelevant for self-managed HOAs. We excluded them from the evaluation.

## Why Board Presidents Need Different Software Than Property Managers

Property managers are paid professionals who manage HOAs as their full-time job. They can justify $500+/month software with deep feature sets and steep learning curves. Volunteer board presidents have day jobs. They need software that handles compliance automatically, not software that requires professional training to operate.
