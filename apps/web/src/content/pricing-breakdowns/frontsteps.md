## Frontsteps does not publish full pricing

Frontsteps does not list a complete price table on its website. Getting an exact number requires a sales conversation. TrustRadius lists a Basic plan at $75/month, but boards evaluating the full HOA software suite should request a written quote that separates each module and implementation cost.

That quote should state whether gate access, amenity reservations, package tracking, work orders, payments, onboarding, and support are included or billed separately. Without that detail, a board cannot compare Frontsteps against flat-rate software or budget the renewal confidently.

## What Frontsteps is designed to do

Frontsteps is built around community access control. The platform's distinguishing capability is gate management: vehicle credentials, visitor logging, entry and exit tracking, and integration with physical gate systems. That access layer sits on top of standard HOA software features, resident portals, violation tracking, online dues collection, and document management.

This combination makes Frontsteps a reasonable fit for gated communities where physical access management is a genuine operational challenge. If credentialing residents, tracking visitor entries, and managing gate hardware requires a dedicated system, Frontsteps addresses that need.

For communities without physical gates, that access management layer adds cost without adding applicable capability.

## The gate access add-on cost

Gate access management is a separate Frontsteps product area. Boards that need vehicle and credential management, visitor tracking, and gate integration should confirm whether those capabilities are included in the quoted package.

The practical risk is modular ambiguity. A board may ask for core management, gate access, amenity reservations, and work orders, then discover that not every capability is included in the first quote.

Before signing a Frontsteps contract, boards should request an itemized quote that shows each module they plan to use and the total monthly cost at their community's unit count. The difference between a core-only quote and a broader package can be significant.

## What Frontsteps does not cover for reserve compliance

Frontsteps published feature descriptions do not include reserve fund tracking, fund separation enforcement, or reserve study target management. For boards in states with mandatory reserve fund laws, California, Florida, Washington, and others, these are not optional features. They are the documentation mechanism that demonstrates compliance and limits personal liability for board directors.

A platform that tracks who drives through the gate but cannot show whether the reserve fund is on track to meet a state-mandated threshold creates a specific gap for boards with statutory obligations. Managing that compliance through a separate spreadsheet while paying per-unit fees for gate access is a workable but inefficient arrangement.

## Quote-led pricing versus flat-rate tools

Quote-led pricing means your software cost is determined during the sales process. The board has to verify which modules are active, what onboarding costs apply, and what happens at renewal.

Flat-rate tools price by community size bracket, not by counting units. Gavelhouse charges $10/month billed annually with Y80OFF for communities up to 50 homes, $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF for 51-200 homes, and $50/month billed annually with Y80OFF for 201-500 homes. A 180-unit community pays $27/month billed annually with Y80OFF regardless of how many included self-serve features it uses.

## Gavelhouse versus Frontsteps for self-managed boards

We built Gavelhouse for the board treasurer managing fund separation and fiduciary financial records, not for the gated community operations manager tracking vehicle credentials. If your community has gates and physical access infrastructure that needs a dedicated management system, Frontsteps addresses that specific need. If your board is primarily concerned with reserve balance visibility, financial reporting, and keeping the community operationally current, you are paying for Frontsteps' gate infrastructure whether you use it or not.
