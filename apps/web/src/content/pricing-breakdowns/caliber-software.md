## No public pricing

Caliber Software does not have a pricing page. The company's website directs all pricing inquiries to a sales contact form. This is the norm for enterprise property management platforms targeting professional management companies - CINC Systems, Vantaca, and Enumerate use the same approach.

For a volunteer board treasurer trying to compare five platforms before a board meeting, quote-based pricing creates an immediate obstacle. You cannot put Caliber into a cost comparison spreadsheet without first scheduling a demo, working through a sales process, and waiting for a custom proposal. By the time you have that number, platforms with transparent pricing have already been evaluated and discussed.

## Who Caliber Software is built for

Caliber Software targets professional property management companies - firms managing portfolios of HOA and condo communities on behalf of clients. The platform's feature set reflects this: portfolio-level reporting, management company workflows, and multi-community accounting tools are central to the product.

This means the pricing assumes a management company that can amortize software costs across a portfolio of client communities. A firm managing 40 HOAs pays one platform fee and spreads it across all 40 accounts. That math works for a management company. It does not work for a single self-managed community paying full enterprise rates.

## What the quote likely covers

Based on Caliber Software's platform positioning and the pricing patterns common among enterprise HOA management software, a quote would typically include:

- **Base platform access:** Monthly or annual subscription covering the core feature set - accounting, owner portals, violations, and work orders
- **Per-community or per-unit pricing:** Enterprise HOA platforms frequently price based on the number of communities managed or units under management
- **Implementation and onboarding:** Initial setup, data migration from prior systems, and staff training are commonly priced separately and can add meaningful first-year cost
- **Module add-ons:** Some platforms charge separately for features like e-payments, communication tools, or advanced reporting

None of these line items are publicly available without contacting Caliber's sales team.

## The self-managed board problem

If you are a volunteer treasurer searching for "Caliber Software pricing," there is a good chance you are evaluating options for a self-managed community. The honest answer: Caliber Software is probably not the right platform to evaluate.

Self-managed boards are not Caliber's target market. The platform's complexity, onboarding requirements, and management company focus make it a poor fit for a volunteer board where the treasurer changes every two or three years and the software needs to be usable without professional property management training.

We built Gavelhouse specifically for this situation. The platform enforces fund separation between operating and reserve accounts at the database level - addressing the commingling risk that QuickBooks and generic accounting tools create. Reserve balances and board-ready financial records stay visible, while state-specific requirements and disclosure language should remain in the board's legal or reserve study review process.

No per-unit fees. No implementation charges. No sales call required. A 30-day trial with a 30-day money-back guarantee gives volunteer boards time to evaluate the platform against actual workflows before committing.

For a self-managed community comparing software options, the total first-year cost difference between a quote-based enterprise platform and a published flat-rate tool can easily run into thousands of dollars - before accounting for implementation and training fees that quote-based platforms typically add on top.

## What to do if you need a Caliber quote

If you are a property management company evaluating Caliber Software for portfolio use, the sales process is the right path. Come prepared with:

- Total number of communities under management
- Total units under management across your portfolio
- Current software and data migration scope
- Required features beyond the base platform
- Whether you need integrations with existing accounting or payment systems

Ask for total first-year cost including implementation and training, not just the recurring subscription rate. The difference between the two numbers often matters more than the base price comparison.
