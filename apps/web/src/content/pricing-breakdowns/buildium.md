## The tiered pricing

Buildium moved from per-unit pricing to flat monthly tiers. Three plans: Essential at $62/mo, Growth at $192/mo, and Premium at $400/mo. The shift to flat tiers looks simpler on the pricing page, but the fees layered on top make the actual cost harder to predict.

## The fees the pricing page buries

Buildium's base prices understate the real cost by 30-50% for a typical HOA:

| Fee type               | Essential         | Growth      | Premium     |
| ---------------------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Incoming EFT           | $2.35/transaction | Reduced     | Reduced     |
| Credit card processing | 2.99%             | 2.99%       | 2.99%       |
| Bank account setup     | $99/account       | $99/account | $99/account |
| eSignature             | $5/document       | Included    | Included    |

For a 100-unit community collecting monthly dues via EFT on the Essential tier:

- Base subscription: $62/mo
- EFT fees: 100 x $2.35 = $235/mo
- Effective monthly cost: $297/mo

That is nearly five times the base price. Growth tier at $192/mo includes reduced EFT fees, making it the more realistic starting point for any community collecting online payments.

## Reserve accounting is a genuine strength

Buildium has full fund accounting with trust accounting and GAAP-recognized reporting. Operating and reserve funds can be properly separated with distinct ledgers and audit trails. This is not partial accounting-level tracking - it is proper fund accounting designed for property management.

The Lumina AI Suite adds AI-powered bill scanning, writing assistance, and conversational AI for support. These features are on the Premium tier and are designed for management companies handling volume.

Buildium was acquired by RealPage in 2019 for $580M. RealPage was subsequently taken private by Thoma Bravo for $10.2B in 2021. The platform manages both rental and HOA properties in one system, with G2 4.4/5 across roughly 220 reviews and Capterra 4.5/5 across roughly 1,969 reviews.

## The self-managed board tradeoff

Buildium's reserve accounting is strong, but the pricing, complexity, and target audience create a mismatch for self-managed volunteer boards. A treasurer on a 100-unit community is looking at $297+/mo on Essential or $192+/mo on Growth. Gavelhouse covers enforced fund separation and reserve balance visibility at about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for communities up to 200 units.
