## The per-unit pricing model

Vinteum charges per unit per month across three tiers:

| Tier     | Per-unit cost | 50 units | 100 units | 200 units | 500 units |
| -------- | ------------- | -------- | --------- | --------- | --------- |
| Basic    | $0.79/unit    | $40      | $79       | $158      | $395      |
| Standard | $0.99/unit    | $50      | $99       | $198      | $495      |
| Premium  | $1.99/unit    | $100     | $199      | $398      | $995      |

The per-unit model means costs scale linearly. Every unit added to the community adds to the monthly bill. For growing communities or communities that annex adjacent developments, the cost increases automatically.

## What each tier includes

All tiers include core community management: resident directory, document storage, and community announcements. The tiers differentiate on communication channels and features.

Premium is where Vinteum's distinctive capability lives: 5-channel outreach (push notifications, email, SMS, automated phone calls, and website posts) plus built-in Zoom integration for virtual meetings with up to 300 attendees. Boards paying for Vinteum and not using Premium are missing the feature that makes the platform unique.

## What no tier includes

No Vinteum tier at any price includes native accounting, financial reporting, dues collection (without third-party integration), reserve fund tracking, or compliance tools. These are not limitations of the Basic tier that unlock at Premium. They do not exist in the platform.

Vinteum integrates with QuickBooks for basic financial operations. QuickBooks is a separate subscription starting at $30+/month. QuickBooks itself cannot properly separate HOA operating and reserve funds without manual workarounds.

## The total cost of ownership

A board using Vinteum for communication needs to add a financial tool:

| Scenario                       | Vinteum (100 units) | Financial tool                           | Total/month                              |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Vinteum Premium + QuickBooks   | $199                | $30+                                     | $229+                                    |
| Vinteum Premium + PayHOA       | $199                | $109+                                    | $308+                                    |
| Vinteum Premium + Gavelhouse   | $199                | about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF | $238.50                                  |
| Gavelhouse only (51-200 units) | -                   | about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF | about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF |

The question is whether Vinteum's communication capabilities justify paying for two platforms. For boards where resident engagement is the primary challenge and accounting is already handled, the answer may be yes. For boards that need both communication and financial compliance, a single platform is more cost-effective.

Gavelhouse covers fund-separated financial management and reserve balance visibility at about $10-$50/mo billed annually with Y80OFF flat with no per-unit fees.
