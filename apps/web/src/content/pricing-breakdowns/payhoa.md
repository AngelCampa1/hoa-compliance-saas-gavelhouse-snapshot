## PayHOA's published pricing

PayHOA is one of the more transparent HOA software vendors. Pricing scales by unit count starting at $49/mo for up to 25 units on annual billing. All features are included at every tier - no feature gating, no upsells. The company raised a $27.5M Series A in May 2024, and the platform is purpose-built for HOAs rather than adapted from rental management software.

At 100 units, the effective cost is about $1.09/unit/mo. For communities over 500 units, pricing scales down to roughly $0.55/unit/mo. Start with the 30-day money-back guarantee.

## What the pricing page does not mention

**Payment processing fees.** PayHOA's subscription covers access to online payment features. Processing fees come on top. Every dues payment collected online incurs a transaction cost. For a community where many homeowners pay by card, processing fees can increase the effective cost above the subscription rate.

**Reserve tracking is partial.** PayHOA offers reserve tracking through its accounting module with custom chart of accounts and bank syncing. You can set up reserve categories and connect bank accounts. But there is no dedicated reserve study module, no percent-funded dashboard, and no fund separation enforcement at the transaction level. The accounting tools show your balance; they do not track progress against a reserve study's funding recommendations.

PayHOA's all-features-included model is a strength. The difference is in financial governance: Gavelhouse includes fund separation enforcement and reserve balance visibility, while reserve study tracking and state-specific compliance dashboards should remain in the board's external review process today.
