## The custom pricing problem for HOA boards

HOA boards typically operate on annual budgets approved by homeowners. Adding a new software subscription requires knowing the cost before getting board approval. Condo Control's custom pricing model requires a sales conversation before you have a number to put in a budget proposal.

This creates a sequencing problem: you cannot get board approval without a price, and you cannot get a price without going through a sales process. For a volunteer board running a quarterly meeting schedule, this adds weeks to the evaluation timeline.

## What Condo Control is built for

Condo Control targets mid-to-large condominium buildings where professional management is common and the administrative workload is high. Features like amenity booking, visitor management, package delivery tracking, and concierge request management reflect that environment.

These are real needs for a high-rise condominium with on-site staff. They are not features that a 60-unit townhome HOA managed by volunteers needs or should pay for.

## Feature cost that does not apply to most HOAs

A Condo Control subscription bundles features for the condo management context regardless of whether your community uses them. If your HOA has no amenities to book, no package room, and no visitor parking system, you are paying for software complexity you will never touch.

## The self-managed alternative

For self-managed HOAs that want transparent pricing and features matched to their actual needs, platforms built for the self-managed market are a more direct fit. Gavelhouse publishes pricing by community size: about $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 50 units, about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 200 units, and about $50/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for up to 500 units. Portfolio is custom for larger operators.
