## The flat pricing

RunHOA's pricing model is the simplest in the HOA software market: $399/year. That is the entire pricing page. No tiers, no per-unit add-ons, no feature gating based on payment level, no user limits. Every community pays the same regardless of size.

The effective monthly cost is $33.25. For perspective:

- A 50-unit community: $0.67/unit/month
- A 100-unit community: $0.33/unit/month
- A 200-unit community: $0.17/unit/month
- A 500-unit community: $0.07/unit/month

At 500 units, RunHOA costs less per unit per month than a postage stamp.

## What the price includes

RunHOA bundles a comprehensive feature set into the flat price. HOA-specific accounting with budgeting and financial reporting. Online dues collection. E-voting for board resolutions. Amenity reservation management. Community communication tools. And 1120-H tax form generation, which is the IRS form specifically for HOA tax filings.

Reserve tracking is available through the accounting module. You can create separate fund categories for operating and reserve accounts. There is no dedicated reserve study module or state-specific compliance tools.

## The hidden costs question

RunHOA's platform fee is $399/year with no visible add-ons. Payment processing for online dues collection involves separate fees from the payment processor, but RunHOA does not clearly document these on its pricing page. Boards should confirm payment processing costs before committing.

No implementation fee is advertised. No data migration fee is listed. Whether those costs emerge during onboarding is unknown given the absence of user reviews.

## The risk factor the price creates

A volunteer board's fiduciary duty extends to the tools it selects for managing community money. RunHOA's price is the lowest in the market for a platform with accounting. But the complete absence of third-party reviews on any major platform creates an evaluation gap that price alone cannot fill.

Every other option has some review presence. PayHOA has 70+ reviews. Buildium has nearly 2,000. Boards considering RunHOA should weigh whether the $150-800/year savings over alternatives justifies betting on an unvalidated platform for their financial operations.

Gavelhouse starts at about $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF ($174/year) for up to 50 units with enforced fund separation, reserve balance visibility, and verifiable reliability.
