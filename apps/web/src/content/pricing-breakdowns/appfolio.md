## How AppFolio pricing works

AppFolio charges a monthly minimum plus a per-unit fee. You pay whichever is higher: the minimum or the per-unit calculation. For most HOAs, the per-unit calculation exceeds the minimum once communities reach a certain size.

At 100 units on the Core tier: $280 minimum vs. $1.40 x 100 = $140. The minimum applies. At 200 units on the Plus tier: $375 minimum vs. $3 x 200 = $600. The per-unit calculation applies.

## The professional management company assumption

AppFolio's pricing model assumes a management company that manages dozens of properties and can spread the minimum across multiple clients. A management company with 50 client HOAs paying the Core minimum splits $280 across all of them, netting $5.60 per HOA per month.

A single self-managed HOA pays the full minimum. That is the structural mismatch.

## Feature cost that does not translate to HOA value

The Plus and Max tiers include leasing automation, AI leasing assistants, and revenue management tools. These are rental property management features. A homeowners association does not lease units. Paying for Plus or Max adds cost without adding HOA-relevant functionality.
