## The free tier

HOA Express offers a genuinely free tier for communities up to 50 households. Unlimited website pages, file storage, email blasts, and a community calendar are all included with no time limit. This is not a free trial that expires - it is a permanent free plan.

## The paid tiers

Scaling beyond 50 households requires a paid plan. Inform at $15/mo adds custom domain mapping. Engage at $21/mo includes the full communication suite. Large community pricing goes up to $79/mo for 5,000 households.

## What no tier includes

No HOA Express tier at any price includes accounting, financial reporting, dues collection, violation tracking, reserve fund tracking, compliance tools, or board meeting management. These are not limitations of the free plan. They are not available on any plan.

## The total cost of ownership

A board that needs both a community website and financial management pays for two tools. HOA Express free + Gavelhouse about $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF = about $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for small communities. HOA Express $15/mo + Gavelhouse about $27/mo billed annually with Y80OFF = about $31/mo for 51-200 units. The combined cost may exceed what an all-in-one platform charges.

Gavelhouse starts at about $10/mo billed annually with Y80OFF for communities up to 50 units, with fund separation and reserve balance visibility included.
